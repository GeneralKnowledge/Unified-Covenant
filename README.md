# Unified Covenant

A project to **construct a coherent new Abrahamic religion** (Unified Covenant), grounded in historical reconstruction, comparative analysis, and preservation-first theology — while keeping the chronicle and decision record intellectually honest.

The name comes from [THE_UNIFIED_COVENANT.md](./THE_UNIFIED_COVENANT.md)—a **first speculative essay** (not project doctrine). Historical work may change, complicate, or invalidate parts of that synthesis.

## Three-part method

1. **Historical reconstruction** — chronicle chapters grounded in scholarship, with explicit evidence labels and bibliographies.  
2. **Comparative agreement and disagreement** — structured ledgers of shared inheritance, conflicts, and open questions ([comparison phase](./comparison/README.md)).  
3. **Experimental ideological synthesis** — LLM-assisted merge attempts **only after** sufficient history and comparison (see the essay for an early sketch).
