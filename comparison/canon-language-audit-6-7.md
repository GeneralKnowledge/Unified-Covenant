> **Canonical note:** “Receive” means UC **includes** Tanakh, New Testament, and Qur’an as **layers of witness** under **CCRP** (**DR-06**). It **does not** adopt **DR-10** preservation or *taḥrīf* **B** while **DR-10** remains **C** (**MD-06**).

### §5.1 insert (editorial)

> **Witness (UC):** Each canonical layer is **authoritative for UC** as **scripture-in-community**, interpreted through **CCRP**, without **automatically** endorsing every **R** claim of the originating tradition about inspiration, preservation, or exclusive covenant.

### §1 “preserves inherited revelation” (substantive — maintainer choice)

**Option 2 (keep text):** Add explicit **DR-10** **C** pointer in same sentence.
