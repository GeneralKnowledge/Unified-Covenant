## Anti-circularity

The Constitution **cannot** settle a scriptural dispute by citing **only** its own supremacy clause. UC **B** text is **not** independent proof of historical **H** or inherited **R** claims. **Decision authority** (what UC has decided) **≠** **evidence provenance** (what sources support **H** or **R** claims about the world or traditions).

---

## 0. Four dimensions (do not conflate)

Every sub-question in a CCRP run must be readable on **four separate axes**:

| Dimension | Question | Values (this repo) |
|-----------|----------|-------------------|
| **A — Claim class** | What **kind of proposition** is under examination? | **H**, **R**, **B** (proposed/adopted UC doctrine on merits), **C** (open UC question), **A** (motif convergence). **Governance questions** (method, procedure, pedagogy, language rules) are **not** a substitute for **H/R/B/C/A** on theological or historical **merits**—they are institutional-rule questions answered with **RS-1** kind **G** when settled. |
| **B — RS status** | What is the **procedural status** of this sub-question? | **RS-1** … **RS-4** (**§1.1**) |
| **C — Outcome kind** | If **RS-1**, what **kind of outcome** is already settled? | **B**, **H**, or **G** (**§1.4**). **G is not a claim class** and **does not** establish truth of **H** or **R** claims. |
| **D — Evidence & inference** | What supports substantive claims? | **3A** authority, **3B** **SD-***, **3C** reasoning (**§3**) |

**Register (step 1):** Record **claim class** (or “governance question”) **and** assign **RS** + **outcome kind** (if **RS-1**) **independently**.

## 1. Ruling status (mandatory at opening)

Decompose each dispute into sub-questions **q1…qn**. Assign **one RS code per sub-question**. When **RS-1**, tag **outcome kind** (**§1.4**). A single case may mix **RS-1** (different kinds), **RS-2**, etc.

### 1.1 Definitions

| Code | Meaning | CCRP behaviour |
|------|---------|----------------|
| **RS-1** Settled outcome | An **applicable** approved decision or binding register entry **establishes the outcome** for **this sub-question’s exact scope** | **Do not** re-adjudicate **that scoped outcome**; **still** complete steps 2–4; step 5 **records** outcome with **3A** + **outcome kind** |
| **RS-2** Settled open (**C**) | Decision **explicitly preserves** this sub-question as **unresolved** (substantive **C**) | Full steps 1–4; step 5 **may not** adopt new **substantive B** without **§12** + reopen |
| **RS-3** Reopen candidate | **§12** reopening pending or maintainer accepted **Reasoned challenge** | **Freeze** new **substantive B** or new **H baseline** on **that sub-question** until reopen completes |
| **RS-4** Unaddressed | No applicable settled outcome or open reservation | Full CCRP; step 5 may propose **B** per §10 where permitted |

**Not RS-1:** Merely **citing** an **MD** identifier does **not** make a sub-question **RS-1**. **RS-1** includes substantive **B**/**H** **and** binding governance **G** (**§1.4**)—each on its **own** sub-question only.

### 1.2 How to assign RS (scope test)

For **each** sub-question, apply steps **in order** (first match that applies after step 2 governs status):

1. **Frame** — One-sentence sub-question; record **claim class** (**H/R/B/C/A**) for **merits**, or label **governance question** if the sub-question is only about UC rules/process/language (**§0**).  
2. **Locate** — **Narrowest** governing **MD**, **DR-***, **§**, or prior **§12** ruling whose **approved scope** matches. If **none** and the question is governance-only → candidate **RS-4** (no invented **G** authority).  
3. **Reopen first** — If **§12** reopening is **active** on **this sub-question**, or maintainer has **accepted** a **Reasoned challenge** moving it to reopen → **RS-3** (skip steps 4–6 for status). **Reasoned challenge alone** (not accepted) → **does not** change **RS**; log in step 6.  
4. **Open substantive** — If governing entry **reserves** this sub-question as **C** (e.g. **MD-06**, **DR-10** on preservation theology) → **RS-2**.  
5. **Settled** — If governing entry **settles** this sub-question → **RS-1** + **outcome kind** **B**, **H**, or **G** (**§1.4**). Constitutional **B** wording in v0.4 may describe a **method** while **CCRP** classifies the operational requirement as **G**—both can be true on different dimensions (**traceability**).  
6. **Unaddressed** — Else → **RS-4**.

**Mixed disputes:** **RS-1 G** (“must run CCRP?”) **coexists** with **RS-2** (preservation theology) as **separate sub-questions**—neither determines the other.

**Precedence when sources conflict:** Approved **MD** → Constitution **B/H** text → traceability (audit map only). **Stop** step 5 and publish maintainer reconciliation memo if they disagree.

### 1.4 Outcome kind (required when **RS-1**)

Tag each **RS-1** sub-question with exactly one **outcome kind**:

| Kind | Settles | Does **not** settle |
|------|---------|---------------------|
| **B** | UC **substantive doctrine** for this sub-question | **H**, **R**, or **C** on other sub-questions |
| **H** | UC **historical baseline** for this sub-question | **B** vindication theology, **R** readings, or **C** topics |
| **G** | **Governance / procedure / pedagogy / language** within approved **MD** scope | Substantive truth of **H**/**R**/**C** claims under review |

**Examples:** **MD-07** preservation-first **method** → **RS-1**, kind **G**; **DR-10** preservation theology → **RS-2**. **MD-11** CCRP binding → **RS-1**, kind **G** on “must this dispute follow CCRP?” — **not** kind **B** on the dispute’s theology.

**Reopen / freeze:** **RS-3** freezes new **substantive B** and new **H baseline** on the challenged sub-question. Changing binding **G** rules (e.g. CCRP text) requires **§12** / maintainer path for procedure amendments—not an ordinary CCRP step-5 override.

### 1.5 Maintainer decisions — scope and outcome kind (not a blanket RS-1 list)

| ID | Typical **RS-1** sub-questions (kind) | **RS-2** or out of scope |
|----|----------------------------------------|---------------------------|
| **MD-01** | God / Jesus UC doctrine (**B** — **DR-01**, **DR-02**) | Nicene or Muslim **R** as UC **B** |
| **MD-02** | Crucifixion (**H** — **DR-03**); vindication (**B** — **DR-04**) | **R** “reconciliation” of 4:157 with passion |
| **MD-03** | Canon layers (**B** — **DR-06**); conflicts use CCRP (**G** — **DR-07**) | Auto-settling every clash without CCRP |
| **MD-04** | UC seal of canonical series (**B** — **DR-08**) | Dominant Islamic **R** finality as UC **B** |
| **MD-05** | Non-supersession community (**B** — **DR-09**) | UC replacing J/C/I covenantal identity |
| **MD-06** | — | Preservation / *taḥrīf* theology → **RS-2** (**DR-10**) |
| **MD-07** | Preservation-first **method** (**G**) | All **R** claims reconciled or preserved |
| **MD-08** | Official teaching accuracy; §7.3 boundaries (**G**) | Universal worship affirmation; comprehensive belief test |
| **MD-09** | Comparative *taḥrīf* pedagogy + **SD-*** (**G**) | **DR-10** theological **B** |
| **MD-10** | Canon-reception / witness **language** (**G**) | Source traditions accepting UC interpretation |
| **MD-11** | This CCRP as binding procedure (**G**) | Truth of claims examined under CCRP |

**Reasoned challenge:** Logged in step 6; **does not** suspend **RS-1** unless maintainer moves sub-question to **RS-3**. **RS-1** **does not** block evidence review—it blocks **silent overturn** of the **scoped** outcome (including **G** within scope).

## 2. Canon authority modes

| Mode | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Inclusion (B)** | Text listed in Unified Canon layers (**DR-06**) |
| **Witness (B + procedural)** | Layer used in UC narrative, worship, CCRP—**layer-specific function** |
| **UC doctrine (B)** | Binds members after **MD** / **§12** |
| **Source-tradition (R)** | Authority **inside** Judaism, Christianity, Islam |
| **Binding UC teaching** | CCRP outcome with **binding** flag for **official** instruction |

**Receive:** UC **includes** canonical **witnesses** under CCRP; **does not** adopt every **R** claim of inspiration, inerrancy, or *taḥrīf*; **does not** resolve **DR-10** while **C**. UC recognition **≠** source traditions’ recognition of UC interpretation.

## 3. Decision authority, evidence provenance, and inference

Every step-4 option and step-5 record must separate **three** layers. **Never** use layer **A** as a substitute for **B** or **C**.

### 3A — Decision authority (what UC has decided)

Record for each **RS-1**, **RS-2**, or governing sub-question:

- **Identifier:** **MD-***, **DR-***, **§**, or prior **§12** ruling  
- **RS code** and **outcome kind** (**B** / **H** / **G** when **RS-1**)  
- **Scope:** Exact sub-question governed  
- **Status / reopen:** Per docket and §12  

This is **project authority**, not **SD-*** verification of scripture or history.

**Documentary sub-question only:** If the dispute is “**what** did UC decide?” the decision text may be read at **SD-PRI** **for that documentary claim alone**.

### 3B — Evidence provenance (**SD-*** on sources)

**Claim classes:** **H**, **R**, **A** (motif only), **B**, **C**. **Never** use **A** for bibliographic depth.

| **SD-*** | Meaning (reading ≠ endorsement) |
|----------|----------------------------------|
| **SD-PRI** | Complete **primary** text read (scripture, inscription, etc.—as relevant to the claim) |
| **SD-ART** | Complete scholarly article or monograph chapter read |
| **SD-EXC** | Substantial excerpt |
| **SD-ABS** | Abstract, metadata, or publisher summary only |
| **SD-SEC** | Secondary summary |
| **SD-EXI** | Existence or bibliography verified; content **not** inspected |
| **SD-INA** | Inaccessible or relevant passage unverified |

**Depth ≠ reliability:** **SD-PRI** does not make every claim from that text true. **SD-SEC** can still support a cautious **H** or **R** survey entry.

**Rules:**

- Approved **B**/**H** **does not** prove **H** about past events or **R** inside Judaism/Christianity/Islam.  
- Reading **MD**/**DR**/**§** **does not** count as **SD-PRI** for the **subject matter** of that decision (only for narrow “what UC decided” questions).  
- **RS-2** → **no new B** on that sub-question regardless of **SD-***.

**Decisive step-4 minimums (evidence for claims under examination):**

| Claim under examination | Minimum **SD-*** for a **decisive** option leg |
|-------------------------|-----------------------------------------------|
| **H** (past event) | **SD-EXC** from **H** scholarship or **SD-PRI** if claim is text-internal |
| **H** (manuscript) | **SD-ART** or **SD-EXC** handbook, or labelled **SD-SEC** matrix row |
| **R** (dominant) | **SD-EXC** normative text or **SD-SEC** survey naming stream |
| **R** (minority) | **SD-EXC** or **SD-SEC** **if attested** — omit if not established |
| **Existing UC outcome (RS-1)** | **3A** citation + outcome kind — **separate** **SD-*** table for subject-matter **H**/**R** evidence |

**Mixed bundles:** Decisive evidential weight = weakest **decisive** leg; **SD-EXI** / **SD-INA** **cannot** support **new B** or high-confidence **H**/**R** conclusions. Label **unequal verification**—no false parity.

**DR-10 topics (unchanged strictness):** High-confidence **R** on *taḥrīf*/preservation requires **SD-EXC** primary tafsīr or **SD-ART** monograph **on that claim**; else **low-confidence** mandatory.

### 3C — Argument and inference

For each option: state how **3B** sources support the claim, assumptions, interpretive steps, counterevidence, and remaining uncertainty. **3A** (**RS-1**, any kind) **constrains** UC conduct or teaching **within scope** but **cannot** replace **3B** for tradition or history claims—and **G** **never** substitutes for substantive **B**/**H** evidence on the merits.

## 4. Steps 1–6

1. **Register** — Passages, translation, genres, claim types, **RS** + **outcome kind** per sub-question (§1.2–§1.4).  
2. **Context** — Language, setting, intertexts, chronicle/registry **H** limits.  
3. **Tradition survey** — Dominant **R**, attested minority, period/school, **strongest objection**, **ranking uncertainty** (**SD-*** cited).  
4. **Publish alternatives** — ≥2 serious options; **3A** authority + **3B** **SD-*** table + **3C** inference; conflicting scholarship **both** listed.  
5. **UC ruling proposal** — **B** text if permitted, costs, classification; maintainer/council per §10; **MD-05** supersession optics.  
6. **Record** — Traceability ID; **dissent**; **3A**/**3B**/**3C** summary; revision trigger.

## 5. Outcomes and deadlock

| Outcome | Binds members? |
|---------|----------------|
| Theological **C** | No **B** on topic |
| **Official UC teaching** | Yes, when **binding** flag set |
| **UC practice rule** | **B** + §12 if new |
| **Private inherited R** | No if §7.3 allows; **official** teaching must respect essential **H**/**B** |
| **Constitutional amendment** | §12 only |

**Deadlock:** No consensus → default **C**; no new **B** on **RS-2**. **H vs R** on past events: **H** binds history curriculum; **R** taught comparatively. **Split council:** **B** only with minority report.

**Pedagogy (**MD-09**):** Comparative teaching + **SD-*** assessment + explicit uncertainty—**not** DR-10 **B** while **MD-06** open.

**Teaching minimum (**MD-08**):** Official teachers present UC **H**/**B** accurately and **R** diversity without misrepresentation—**not** a universal worship affirmation test.
