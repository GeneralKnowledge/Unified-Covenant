# Phase 6.6 — CCRP adversarial stress test

**Status:** Evaluation report. **Not** UC doctrine. **Does not** amend [unified-covenant-v0.3.md](./unified-covenant-v0.3.md) or **MD-01–MD-06**.

**Baseline procedure:** v0.3 §5.3 CCRP (six steps + deadlock table). **Anti-circularity:** Constitution **cannot** settle by **only** citing supremacy.

**Related research:** [Phase 6.5 taḥrīf](../research/phase-6-5-tahrif-and-scriptural-preservation.md) · [Phase 6.6 sources](../research/phase-6-6-source-verification.md) · [traceability](./unified-covenant-v0.3-traceability.md)

**Classification:** **H** · **R** · **A** · **B** · **C** · **Procedural**

---

## Executive summary

CCRP, **as written**, can **register**, **classify**, and **either** adopt **B** **or** **default** **to** **C** on hard conflicts **without** a single-step “Constitution wins” circularity. **However**, **maintainer** **rulings** **MD-01–MD-06** **already** **embed** **outcomes** for Cases **B–D** in **constitutional** **text**, so a **naïve** CCRP run **risk** **re-litigating** **settled** **B**/**H** or **pretending** **R** **streams** **were** **reconciled**.

**Strongest aspect:** Deadlock → **C** + **H** vs **R** split on past events (§5.3) prevents **manuscript** **H** **alone** **from** **canceling** **inherited** **R** **in** **comparative** **teaching**.

**Most serious weakness:** **Layer** **4** **(Constitution)** **plus** **pre-rulings** **can** **short-circuit** steps **4–5** **in** **practice**; **“receive”** **canon** **language** **without** **DR-10** **B** **invites** **incompatible** **member** **expectations** (Case **A**, **E**).

**Overall:** **No** **case** **rated** **Fail** **for** **procedure** **alone**; **four** **Qualified** **pass**, **one** **Pass** **(Case** **A** **procedural** **default)**. **No** **new** **doctrine** **proposed** **as** **adopted**.

## Case A — Scriptural preservation and *taḥrīf*

### A.0 Disputed proposition

**What UC must adjudicate under CCRP (if at all):** Whether UC **teaches** **any** **binding** **claim** about **corruption** **or** **preservation** **of** **Tanakh/NT** **relative** **to** **Qur’anic** **accusations**.

**Pre-ruling:** **MD-06** → **DR-10** **C**; **no** **new** **B** **without** **§12**.

### Step 1 — Register conflict

| Passage | Claim type | Proposition |
|---------|------------|-------------|
| Q **2:75–79**, **3:78**, **4:46**, **5:13–15**, **5:41**, **5:43–48** | **R** (Qur’an) + **R** (Jewish/Christian preservation) + **H** (mss) | See [Phase 6.5 §3](../research/phase-6-5-tahrif-and-scriptural-preservation.md) |
| UC Declaration | **B** | “Receive Tanakh, NT, Qur’an as Unified Canon” |
| DR-10 | **C** | Preservation theology **open** |

**Translation (study gloss, verified Phase 6.6):** e.g. Q 2:75 — party **distort** Torah **after** understanding; Q 5:43 — they come for judgment while **Torah** contains Allāh’s judgment; Q 5:13 — **distort words** from proper places ([source report](../research/phase-6-6-source-verification.md)).

### Step 2 — Context pass

- **Genre:** Polemic, covenant lawsuit, legal disputation (**R** address to **7th-c.** communities — **H** **contested** **identities**).
- **Internal Qur’anic tension:** Accusatory **ḥ-r-f** **vs** **5:43–48** **affirming** **Torah/Injīl** **as** **guidance** — **real** **tension**, **not** **apparent**.
- **Category error risk:** Collapsing **kitmān**, **maʿnā**, **naṣṣ**, and **forged** **writing** (2:79) into one **doctrine** — **procedure** **must** **split** (**Phase** **6.5**).

### Step 3 — Tradition survey (strongest **R**, with diversity)

| Stream | Position | Internal diversity |
|--------|----------|-------------------|
| **Islam** | **Textual** *taḥrīf* **(popular)**; **maʿnā** **(classical** **Ṭabarī** **strand,** **S**); **partial** **reliability** **(5:43–48** **apologetics)** | **I-1** **vs** **I-3** in [6.5 §4](../research/phase-6-5-tahrif-and-scriptural-preservation.md) |
| **Judaism** | **Providential** **preservation** **of** **Torah** **(written** **+** **oral** **R)** | **Critical** **vs** **Orthodox** **on** **inerrancy** |
| **Christianity** | **Inspired** **scripture**; **textual** **variants** **acknowledged** **(mainstream** **academic** **Christianity)** | **Inerrantist** **vs** **critical** |

### Step 4 — Publish alternatives (minimum two)

| Option | Summary | Cost |
|--------|---------|------|
| **A1 — No UC B (MD-06)** | Teach **H** variants; **R** **voices**; **C** **DR-10** | **Ambiguous** “receive” |
| **A2 — Limited distortion B** | UC **affirms** **interpretive/concealment** **charges** **only** | **Jewish** **objection** **risk** |
| **A3 — Substantial preservation B** | UC **affirms** **continuity** **of** **witness** **texts** | **Popular** **Muslim** **textual** **taḥrīf** **cost** |

### Step 5 — UC ruling proposal

**Under current constitution:** **No** **new** **B** **permitted** **without** **overturning** **MD-06**. **Proposal:** **Maintain** **C**; **publish** **A1** **as** **operative**.

### Step 6 — Record

- **Traceability:** DR-10 **unchanged**; link Phase 6.5 **+** **6.6**.
- **Dissent:** Members **may** **hold** **private** **R** **on** **taḥrīf** **if** **public** **teaching** **respects** **MD-06** **distinctions** (implicit from §11; **not** **litmus** **for** **membership**).
