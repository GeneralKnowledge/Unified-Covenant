## A. Executive assessment

**Judgement:** v0.4 is **coherent in its core but materially incomplete** as a **lived religion**, with **one traceability/documentation inconsistency** and **several underdefined zones**—not a demonstration that Judaism, Christianity, and Islam already agree.

**Why not “fully coherent religion”:**

- **Positive theology exists** (One Sovereign, Anointed, vindication, canon series, ethics motifs) and **layers H/R/B/C** are mostly explicit (**MD-02**, **MD-04**, **MD-06**).
- **Worship, liturgy, sacraments, daily prayer, and detailed soteriology** are deliberately **open** (§8, §13 UQ-02/03)—so UC reads as a **constitutional covenant + creed** more than a complete **cultus**.
- **Canon in practice** depends on **CCRP** execution; anti-circularity is **procedural**, not a guarantee that conflicting **R** witnesses feel “equally authoritative” to members from each background.

**Why not “internally inconsistent”:**

- No found passage that **requires** Nicene Trinity **and** denies it; **requires** literal 4:157 **and** **H** crucifixion as **B**; or **closes DR-10** while **MD-06** open.
- **Tension** (not flat contradiction): §1 promises “**one coherent worship**” while §7.3/§8 defer liturgy—**aspiration vs implementation gap**, not logical impossibility.

**Why not “insufficient to assess”:**

- **MD-01–MD-11** and §14 **DR** register give enough **B/H** anchors to test coherence.

**Examples:**

| Example | Type |
|---------|------|
| Vindication **B** without full atonement **B** (§4.5 vs UQ-03) | **Underdefined**, not contradictory |
| §14 points to v0.3 traceability file | **Documentation omission** |
| DR-10 **C** + three-layer canon **B** | **Inherited disagreement + open UC question**, handled per **MD-06** |

## D. Adversarial findings (selected)

### D.1 Vindication without soteriology

| Field | Content |
|-------|---------|
| **Proposition** | **B** “Vindication of the Anointed” binds members; atonement theory **open** (§4.5, UQ-03). |
| **Why it matters** | A **religion** needs some account of **how** the cross and exaltation relate to forgiveness/obligations—not only that God “vindicated.” |
| **Affected** | §4, §9, §14 **DR-04**; **Christian R** (soteriology); **Muslim R** (no cross soteriology). |
| **Strongest objection** | **Nicene Christian:** UC smuggles Easter **function** without **R** content; **Muslim:** centrality of crucified agent is **shirk**-adjacent. |
| **Strongest UC response** | **MD-02** explicitly **constructive B**, not **R** harmony; vindication as **faith act** and prototype (§4.5, §9). |
| **Evidence vs commitment** | **H** crucifixion supported; **B** vindication is **UC commitment**, not provable **H**. |
| **Costs** | Full atonement **B** → deeper **MD-01/02** reopen risk; leave open → thin **worship** narrative. |
| **Research** | Theological commission (**UQ-03**) before liturgy **MD**. |

## F. Readiness assessment

Before UC is reasonably described as a **more complete coherent religion** (not merely a constitution):

1. **Resolve or deliberately bracket UQ-03** so vindication **B** connects to member **forgiveness/hope** practice.
2. **Minimal liturgy / prayer / calendar **B**** (**UQ-02**) aligned with **MD-08**.
3. **Either** maintain **DR-10 C** with explicit pastoral theology of “witness under dispute” **or** follow ranked task #3.
4. **Living CCRP outputs** (published case records) proving canon procedure works beyond documents.
5. **Catechesis** materials using **MD-09** without implying **DR-10** closure.

A **well-written constitution ≠ fully developed religion**; v0.4 is **strong on epistemics and creed**, **weak on cultus and soteriological detail**.
