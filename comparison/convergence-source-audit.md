Revisit compatibility **outcomes**; narrow overbroad wording; add registry entries only with verifiable references already in chapter bibliographies or primary texts cited there.

**Source standard (project):**

| Field | Requirement |

| Secondary | Academic works listed in Ch.1–4 bibliographies (no invented page numbers) |

| Limits | **Contested** or **Religious tradition** where applicable |
