# Unified Covenant — Glossary (v0.4)

**Purpose:** Clarify specialist terms used in [unified-covenant-v0.4.md](./unified-covenant-v0.4.md) and [ccrp-adopted.md](./ccrp-adopted.md). **Does not** replace constitutional **B** text or CCRP rules in the main documents.

---

## Four dimensions (CCRP — Phase 6.13)

Do **not** conflate: **(1) claim class** of the proposition examined · **(2) RS status** · **(3) RS-1 outcome kind** · **(4) 3A/3B/3C** evidence. See [ccrp-adopted.md §0](./ccrp-adopted.md#0-four-dimensions-do-not-conflate).

---

## Claim classes (proposition under examination)

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **H** | Historical proposition—past events, manuscripts, composition (evidence via **3B**) |
| **R** | Inherited religious claim within Judaism, Christianity, or Islam |
| **A** | Motif convergence (not bibliographic “abstract”) |
| **B** | **Substantive** UC doctrine proposed or adopted **on merits** (membership creed, §14 essentials, etc.) |
| **C** | Substantive UC question **left open** |

**Governance questions** (must UC follow CCRP? must teachers present **R** fairly? which **SD-*** for pedagogy?) are **not** a sixth letter class on **H/R** truth—they are **institutional** sub-questions. When settled, they use **RS-1** with outcome kind **G**, not **B** on preservation theology or **H** on history.

**Register class vs outcome kind:** v0.4 may label **MD-07**–**MD-10** with constitutional **B** wording while CCRP assigns **G** to the operational sub-question—see [traceability](./unified-covenant-v0.4-traceability.md) MD table.

## Source depth (**SD-***)

Verification level for a cited source in CCRP step 4. **Reading ≠ endorsement.**

| Code | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **SD-PRI** | Complete primary text read |
| **SD-ART** | Complete scholarly article or monograph chapter |
| **SD-EXC** | Substantial excerpt |
| **SD-ABS** | Abstract or metadata only |
| **SD-SEC** | Secondary summary |
| **SD-EXI** | Existence verified; content not inspected |
| **SD-INA** | Inaccessible or passage unverified |

---

## Ruling status (**RS-***)

Per **sub-question** at CCRP opening. **Not** determined by listing **MD-01–MD-11** globally—apply the **scope test** in [ccrp-adopted.md §1.2](./ccrp-adopted.md#12-how-to-assign-rs-scope-test).

| Code | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **RS-1** | **Settled outcome** for sub-question scope (requires **outcome kind**) |
| **RS-2** | Sub-question **explicitly open (C)** (e.g. **MD-06**, **DR-10**) |
| **RS-3** | Reopening active—freeze new **substantive B** / **H baseline** on that sub-question |
| **RS-4** | Unaddressed |

## RS-1 outcome kind (Phase 6.12)

| Kind | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **B** | Substantive UC **doctrine** settled for this sub-question |
| **H** | UC **historical baseline** settled for this sub-question |
| **G** | **Governance / procedure / pedagogy / language** rule settled—**does not** settle substantive theology or history under review |

## Decision authority vs evidence (**3A / 3B / 3C**)

| Layer | Role |
|-------|------|
| **3A** | Which **MD**/**DR**/**§** governs; scope; reopen—**project authority** |
| **3B** | **SD-*** codes on sources used to support **H** or **R** claims |
| **3C** | Argument, counterevidence, uncertainty |

**MD**/**DR** text is **not** **SD-PRI** for the subject matter of the decision (except narrow “what UC decided” documentary questions).

## Canon authority modes (**MD-10**)

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Inclusion** | Text is listed in the Unified Canon (**B**) |
| **Canonical witness** | UC uses the text in narrative, worship, and CCRP with **layer-specific** function (Tanakh covenant arc / Anointed witness / *tawḥīd* and seal)—**not** identical to each tradition’s **R** authority mode |
| **Receive (Declaration)** | UC **includes** witnesses and interprets under **CCRP**; **does not** adopt every **R** claim about inspiration, inerrancy, or preservation; **does not** imply source traditions accept UC interpretation |
| **Source-tradition authority** | Oral Torah, Nicene faith, *ijmāʿ*, etc.—**R** **inside** inherited communities |

---

## Preservation-first (**MD-07**)

**Defensible:** A claim survives careful textual, historical, linguistic, theological, and interpretive analysis per §2.1 method—**not** a guarantee that all **R** claims reconcile, and **not** a **B** ruling on **DR-10** while **C**.

---

## Teaching layers (**MD-08**)

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Official UC doctrine** | **§14** essentials + CCRP **binding** teaching |
| **Accurate presentation** | Required of **official** teachers/representatives |
| **Public worship** | No phase-6.10 requirement that every member affirm all **B** in every service |
| **Private belief** | May differ where §7.3 allows |
| **Recorded dissent** | Permitted; must not contradict essential **H**/**B** in **official** teaching |

---

## *Taḥrīf* pedagogy (**MD-09**)

Comparative instruction on preservation, alteration, and interpretation across traditions, with **SD-*** discipline and stated uncertainty—**method only**; **MD-06** / **DR-10** remain **C**.
