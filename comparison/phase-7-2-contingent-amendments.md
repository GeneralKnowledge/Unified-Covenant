## § A — Option A draft (vindication-only clarification)

### §4.5 addendum (after “Defined terms” table)

> **Soteriological bracket:** UC **B** vindication **does not** specify **how** sins are remitted through the cross. UC **does not** teach **penal substitution**, **divine satisfaction**, or **blood sacrifice** as **binding B**. Members seek **forgiveness** from **One Sovereign** through **repentance** and faithful life; the Anointed’s **vindication** **witnesses** that God **does not** abandon the faithful to final shame.

### §9 addendum (after “Not claimed as A”)

> **Forgiveness and the cross:** Personal **forgiveness** is **not** asserted to occur **by mechanism X** disclosed in UC **B**; the **Anointed’s** passion and **vindication** **model** trust under affliction and **ground hope** that God **vindicates** the faithful, without requiring Nicene or substitutionary **R** formulas.

## § B — Option B draft (representative suffering **B** → v0.5)

### New §4.7 (v0.5)

> **Solidarity of the Anointed (B — MD-12):** In the **historical suffering** of Jesus the Anointed on the cross, **One Sovereign** was **faithful** to the obedient servant and **disclosed solidarity with the afflicted**. UC members are **called** to **embody mercy and justice** in the world **in continuity with** this pattern. This **B** **does not** teach that God **needed** the death of a divine Son to forgive sins, **does not** teach **penal substitution**, and **does not** require bodily resurrection **R** formulas beyond **vindication B**.
