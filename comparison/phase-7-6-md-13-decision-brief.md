## 1. Executive summary — decision the maintainer must make

**MD-13** asks whether Unified Covenant should adopt **any binding worship form (B)**—and if so, **how much** of gathering shape, prayer structure, scripture use, and annual calendar becomes **member-binding**, without violating **MD-05** (non-supersession), **MD-08** (no universal worship-affirmation or comprehensive belief test), **MD-03** / **MD-10** (canon layers and witness language), or **MD-12** (vindication-only atonement boundaries).

The maintainer is **not** being asked to pick decorative details. The core choice is **constitutional**:

| Decision axis | Question |
|---------------|----------|
| **Binding level** | **B** liturgy/calendar · **B** minimal assembly only · **G** framework only · **DEFER** (leave **UQ-02** open) |
| **Archetype (if any)** | **Option 1** shared script · **Option 2** layer rotation · **Option 3** minimal covenant assembly |
| **Calendar** | Whether **MD-13** includes annual rhythm **B** or defers calendar facets to **MD-14** / later |
| **Subordinate** | **MD-14** (weekly gathering day) remains **separate** unless maintainer explicitly folds it into **MD-13** |

**This phase approves nothing.** It packages trade-offs so a maintainer can answer the [decision block](#11-maintainer-decision-block) and trigger downstream implementation (§12).

## 3. Options comparison — three MD-13 archetypes

Criteria used for every archetype: **MD-05** optics · **MD-08** fit · **MD-03/10** canon honesty · **MD-06/DR-10** bracket · **MD-12** atonement boundaries · §1 “coherent worship” · operational cost · constitutional footprint (**B** vs **G** vs optional).

### Option 1 — Shared Abrahamic prayer script

**Identity (unchanged):** Weekly gathering with **new UC-composed** wording (monotheism + mercy idioms **inspired by** inherited forms, not copied as **R** obligations), scripture rotation, silence, justice–mercy commitment, closing blessing. See [sketch §3 Archetype 1](../research/phase-7-3-liturgy-and-calendar-sketch.md).

| Criterion | Assessment |
|-----------|------------|
| **Genuinely UC vs blend** | **Mixed.** Fixed script can read as a **distinct UC rite** if language is original and covenants are explicit; risk of **patchwork** if leaders borrow recognizable Jewish/Christian/Muslim formulas without reframing. |
| **One religion without one theology** | **Sensitive.** Coherent **UC** voice helps; without careful **MD-05** framing, marketing can imply “finally one Abrahamic service,” which contradicts non-supersession. |
| **Respectful inherited use** | Requires **MD-08**-grade context (what is **R** in source communities vs **B** in UC); **MD-10** “witness” language for readings. |
| **Imitation / replacement risk** | **Highest** of the three ([sketch: medium MD-05 risk](../research/phase-7-3-liturgy-and-calendar-sketch.md)). |
| **Binding vs optional** | Entire script could be **G** (recommended) or **B** (uniform UC worship) — **B** version increases supersession optics and **MD-08** tension (members who cannot in conscience repeat a fixed prayer). |
| **Theological consequences** | Strongest claim to §1 “coherent worship”; may imply UC **is** a single liturgical church even when theology remains layered. |
| **Operational costs** | Moderate leader training; translation choices; periodic CCRP review of contentious passages. |
| **Community effects** | High identity formation for small groups; may alienate dual-identity members or those attached to **R** liturgies elsewhere. |
| **Constitutional** | Full script as **B** likely needs **§8** calendar/gathering text + possible **DR-14** + **§12** revision record; **G**-only needs CCRP case record + policy memo, not necessarily DR row. |
| **Evidence gaps** | No repository-held ethnographic proof that a composite script “works”; **design assessment only**. Further research: pilot congregations, Jewish/Muslim/Christian advisory feedback (not in repo). |

### Option 2 — Layer-rotating liturgy

**Identity (unchanged):** Recurring cycle (e.g. Tanakh-forward / NT-forward / Qur’an-forward weeks) with shared UC ethical/theological frame; **not** claiming any week **is** a Jewish, Christian, or Muslim service. See [sketch §3 Archetype 2](../research/phase-7-3-liturgy-and-calendar-sketch.md) and exemplar **W2** [ccrp-case-record-exemplar-worship-canon.md](./ccrp-case-record-exemplar-worship-canon.md).

| Criterion | Assessment |
|-----------|------------|
| **Canon layers honestly** | **Strongest** pedagogical match to **DR-06** / **MD-03** — rotation makes **layered** canon visible. |
| **Teach vs ceremonial tokenism** | **High potential** if each week includes CCRP-backed teaching on **R** context and UC **B** limits; **weak** if rotation is only “theme weeks” without interpretive discipline (**MD-09**-style for hard texts). |
| **Ranking / equivalence risks** | Order and length of weeks can imply **implicit hierarchy** (e.g. NT passion week as “center” may echo Christian **R** calendars; Qur’an-only week may feel tokenizing). |
| **Preparation burden** | **Highest** — leaders need literacy across three corpora, **SD-*** discipline, and **MD-02**/**MD-12** language for passion/vindication week. |
| **Difficult passages** | Reading schedule **must not** settle **4:157**, **DR-10**, or atonement **C**; CCRP records per season recommended. |
| **Binding vs optional** | Rotation as **B** mandates community rhythm; as **G** allows local adaptation (exemplar **Q1** proposal: no **B** requiring all three layers every service). |
| **Theological consequences** | Trains members in UC’s **critical inheritance** model; may still look like “three religions taking turns” to outsiders. |
| **Operational** | Needs documented lectionary (**G**), leader handbook, CCRP publication before contentious seasons. |
| **Constitutional** | **B** rotation rule = new **§8** + likely **DR-14**; **G** rotation = forward-progress implementation without new essential membership claim if **MD-08** preserved. |
| **Evidence gaps** | Exemplar is **template only**; no field trials in repository. |

### Option 3 — Minimal covenant assembly

**Identity (unchanged):** Modest assembly: Declaration / covenant, open prayer, one canon passage under documented **G** rules, silence/reflection, ethical action; no fixed sacraments. See [sketch §3 Archetype 3](../research/phase-7-3-liturgy-and-calendar-sketch.md) and exemplar **W3**.

| Criterion | Assessment |
|-----------|------------|
| **§1 “coherent worship”** | **Weakest** surface coherence — may feel thin or “book club plus service project” unless **B** minimum defines *that this assembly is UC worship*. |
| **Small / dispersed communities** | **Strongest** scalability; lowest imitation of **R** full liturgies. |
| **Recognizable UC practice** | Depends on whether **covenant entry**, **Declaration**, and **accountability** rituals are **B**-defined even when words vary. |
| **Coercion / imitation** | **Lowest** risk to **MD-08** and **MD-05** if assembly is clearly voluntary and non-substitutive. |
| **Lived religion adequacy** | Risk: project stays **under-liturgized** for members seeking shared sacred time; mitigated if **B** requires *some* regular assembly without fixing script. |
| **Binding vs optional** | Natural fit for **Boundary 2** (minimal **B** assembly + flexible detail) or **Boundary 3** (**G** only). |
| **Constitutional** | Minimal **B** might be one short **§8** obligation (“covenant assembly”) without **DR-14** if folded into existing **B** ethics/community; separate **DR-14** if register clarity desired. |
| **Evidence gaps** | Whether “minimal” satisfies long-term retention — **organizational**, not textual; outside repo evidence. |

## 5. Tradition-specific objections (strong, fair — not monolithic)

**Jewish perspectives (varied):**

- A “unified” service can appear to **appropriate** *tefillah* / Torah reading **R** forms without **brit**, **minyan**, or halakhic authority — violating **MD-05** if presented as equivalent worship.
- Rotation that treats Tanakh as “Week A” may **flatten** Oral Torah, calendar, and peoplehood dimensions central to many Jewish **R** self-understandings.
- UC use of *Shema*-adjacent language (Option 1 sketch) triggers **boundary sensitivity** even with new wording.

**Christian perspectives (varied):**

- Without Eucharist or historic liturgical calendar, UC worship “is not church” — objection may be irrelevant to UC’s **MD-05** claim, but affects **ecumenical perception** and member expectations from Christian backgrounds.
- NT-forward weeks centered on passion/**H** may **re-inscribe** cross centrality in ways that feel Protestant-default unless **MD-12** brackets are taught (**vindication**, not substitution **B**).
- Fixed UC scripts may conflict with **Nicene**-formed members’ **R** instincts while UC correctly refuses Nicene **B**.

**Muslim perspectives (varied):**

- Composite prayer resembling *Fatiḥa* / *salāt* patterns risks implying UC has a **sharīʿa**-like **B** prayer — UC must not claim identical *salāt* **B** (exemplar Step 3).
- Qur’an in liturgical rotation alongside crucifixion-centric NT weeks can offend if **4:157** **R** diversity is flattened or if crucifixion **H** is liturgized as triumph over Islamic **R** denial narratives.
- Friday / Ramaḍān proximity in calendar sketches is **politically and spiritually loaded**; **MD-14** should not be smuggled into **MD-13** without explicit choice.

**Internal UC tensions:**

- Members wanting **one clear religion** may push **Boundary 1**; conscience-first members push **Boundary 3** — both cite **MD-08**.
- Leaders may prefer **Option 2** pedagogy while congregations lack capacity — operational failure is not a doctrine problem but can discredit UC.

## 10. Provisional recommendation (not adopted)

**Provisional:** Pursue **Decision Boundary 2** — adopt a **minimal covenant assembly B** (regular UC gathering centered on Declaration/covenant and ethical accountability) with **detailed shape as G** (handbook + CCRP lectionary), using **Archetype 3** as the default **G** template and permitting **Archetype 2** as an approved **G** variant for prepared communities — **not** **Archetype 1** as **B** unless maintainer accepts higher **MD-05** risk.

**Strongest case against this provisional path:**

- May **under-deliver** on “genuinely unified religion” if public practice looks indistinguishable from a study group — §1 credibility and member retention suffer.
- **Boundary 2** still creates a new **B** obligation where **Boundary 3** might suffice until pilots exist.
- **Archetype 2** as **G** only may **confuse** teachers who invested in rotation pedagogy expecting **B** status.

**Strongest case for an alternative (Boundary 1 + Option 2 as B):**

- Best aligns UC public identity with **layered canon** and repeated **CCRP** literacy; matches exemplar **W2** direction — at cost of leader burden and **MD-05**/**ranking** risks.

**Maintainer may reject all provisionals** and choose **DEFER** or any boundary/archetype pairing above.
