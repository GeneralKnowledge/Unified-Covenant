## Maintainer scope (recorded)

**MD-13** includes **consideration** of binding annual UC observances. **Specific names, dates, and rules remain sub-decisions** — listed here as **candidates only**.

---

## Candidate observances

### A — Covenant Renewal Day (proposed **B** candidate)

| Field | Content |
|-------|---------|
| **Purpose** | Annual renewal of UC *brit* identity; entry/reaffirmation of members; public reading of Declaration |
| **Theological rationale** | **DR-09** non-supersession; **§7.1** new community; **MD-05** |
| **Proposed class** | **B** (if adopted) — one distinctive UC holy day |
| **Timing** | **Undecided** — options: fixed civil date (e.g. autumn equinox region), anniversary of local founding, or **G** “first Sabbath of month X” pending **MD-14** interaction |
| **MD-05 concerns** | Must not be marketed as “true Passover/Easter/*ʿĪd*”; avoid calendrical collision language implying replacement |
| **MD-08 concerns** | Renewal assent ≠ comprehensive belief test; allow recorded dissent on non-essential lines |
| **Dependencies** | None on **DR-10**; uses existing Declaration |

### B — Day of Witness to the Afflicted (proposed **G** or **O**)

| Field | Content |
|-------|---------|
| **Purpose** | Focused gathering on justice–mercy (**DR-12**); solidarity without new christological **B** |
| **Theological rationale** | §8 ethics; **MD-12** vindication as hope motif only in homily, not new atonement mechanics |
| **Proposed class** | **G** recommended practice or **O** local |
| **Timing** | **Undecided** — deliberately **not** tied to Christian Holy Week or Ramaḍān as UC obligation |
| **MD-05 concerns** | Lower if not mapped to inherited feasts |
| **MD-08** | Optional participation |

### C — Study days near inherited seasons (proposed **O** only)

| Field | Content |
|-------|---------|
| **Purpose** | Comparative learning when Jewish/Christian/Muslim neighbors observe **R** festivals |
| **Proposed class** | **O** — not UC holy days |
| **Timing** | Proximity optional; **no** claim UC observes Passover/Easter/Ramaḍān as **B** |
| **MD-05** | Explicit “study, not observance” framing required |

---

## Explicitly not proposed as UC **B** holy days

- Passover, Easter/Resurrection Sunday, Ramaḍān/*ʿĪd*, Jewish High Holy Days as **UC** obligations — remain **R** in source communities.
