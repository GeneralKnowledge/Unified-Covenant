## Decision question

Should UC adopt a **universally binding B** rhythm for the **weekly** gathering day, or govern scheduling through **G** / local **O** rules only?

---

## Options compared

| Option | Description | MD-05 / identity | Scheduling / dispersion | Interaction with shared script | Inherited worship risk |
|--------|-------------|------------------|-------------------------|----------------------------------|------------------------|
| **Friday** | Fixed **B** weekly UC day | May echo Muslim Friday prayer **R** optics — UC must disclaim *ṣalāt* equivalence | Hard for some workers | Script usable any day; Friday branding sensitive | “UC Friday mosque” misperception |
| **Sunday** | Fixed **B** weekly UC day | Christian-majority regions may read as “new church Sunday” | Common free day in West | Same script; NT readings more frequent by habit | Supersession optics **medium–high** |
| **Other fixed day** (e.g. Saturday neutral) | **B** rhythm on chosen weekday | Lower mimicry of one tradition if explained | Community-specific | Neutral | Requires teaching why not Shabbat **R** obligation |
| **No universal B weekly day** | **G**: locales schedule; script **B** when gathered | **Lowest** supersession signal | **Best** for dispersed groups | **B** form whenever assembly meets | Does not replace inherited calendars |

---

## Analytical recommendation (not a maintainer ruling)

**Provisional:** Prefer **no universal B weekly day** (**G** scheduling) unless maintainer explicitly accepts optics trade-offs of Friday or Sunday.

**Strongest case against that recommendation:** §1 “coherent worship” may imply a shared rhythm; dispersed members lack identity cues.
