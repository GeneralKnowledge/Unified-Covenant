## 1. Problem

UC **receives** Tanakh, New Testament, and Qur’an as **canonical witnesses** (**ADOPTED B**). **MD-06** leaves preservation theology **C**. Members lack a **shared public teaching** on whether variation implies corruption, whether Qur’anic *taḥrīf* language requires UC to teach textual loss, and how **H** criticism relates to **R** providence claims — without collapsing into polemic or silence.

---

## 2. What historical inquiry supports (**H** — UC teaching about evidence, not binding metaphysics)

| Finding | UC may teach as **H** |
|---------|------------------------|
| Manuscript diversity | Hebrew, Greek, and Arabic textual traditions show **variants**; no layer is letter-identical across all witnesses. |
| Canon development | **Collections** of books stabilized over time; “scripture” is not one frozen scroll in the 1st century. |
| Translation layers | Meaning moves through language; UC reads **fixed study translations** under **CCRP**, not raw archetypes. |
| Qur’anic *ḥ-r-f* usage | Accusations in the Qur’an are **contextually diverse** (misplacement of words, concealment, covenant breach, legal rivalry) — **not** one uniform doctrine of wholesale physical erasure of Torah/Gospel (**H** on usage; **R** on later systematisation). |
| Pre-Islamic continuity | Manuscript lines for MT and NT traditions extend **before** Islam; **H** does not support “entire Torah/Gospel invented in the 7th century.” |

**H does not alone prove or disprove God’s providence** (**MD-06** rule preserved).

---

## 3. What UC does **not** adopt as **B**

- **Wholesale *taḥrīf* polemic** — that Jews or Christians **systematically replaced** their scriptures so that **no** covenant instruction remains in Tanakh/NT as UC receives them.
- **Denial of *taḥrīf* as meaningful category** — pretending Muslim **R** concerns about misuse of scripture are merely ignorant.
- **Nicene inerrancy / Muslim letter-perfect *naṣṣ* theology** for Tanakh or NT as UC **B**.
- **UC adjudication** of every **R** claim about inspiration, oral Torah, or Qur’anic mode of revelation.

---

## 4. Recommended binding formulation — **Sufficient Witness** (Option C refined)

**Preferred UC **B** text (proposed):**

> **Sufficient Witness.** One Sovereign has preserved **adequate continuity** in the Tanakh and New Testament as **Unified Canon receives them**, and in the Qur’an as **Unified Canon receives it**, so that together they remain **fit witnesses** for covenant faith, moral instruction, and worship under **CCRP**. Textual variation, translation, and interpretive disagreement **do not** make any canonical layer **morally useless** or **wholly unreliable** as witness. Qur’anic language about distortion and concealment testifies to **misuse and covenant failure** in history (**R** witness UC **respects**); UC **does not** teach that those passages, read as UC **B**, require belief in **wholesale fabrication** of the earlier layers. Where **H** and **R** conflict on preservation, UC **teaches** the distinction; **B** rests on **covenant reception**, not on a claim that every manuscript is identical to an unknown autograph.

### 4.1 What this affirms

- UC’s **three-layer canon** is **trustworthy for its stated purpose** (witness under procedure).
- **Crucifixion **H**** and other **binding historical findings** remain coherent with canon trust.
- **Interpretive** and **legal** abuse of scripture is real (aligned with much classical *taḥrīf al-maʿnā* emphasis).

### 4.2 What this does not affirm

- Letter-perfect preservation of autographs for Tanakh/NT/Qur’an.
- That any one tradition’s **R** preservation theology is **fully** correct in UC’s sense.
- That **no** passage in received texts reflects later editorial layers (**H** continues to inform study).

### 4.3 What remains **C** / open

- Detailed **Injīl** ↔ canonical Gospels mapping in **R** Islam vs UC usage.
- Verse-level rules when **H** reconstruction and **R** reading diverge sharply (handled case-by-case in **CCRP**).
- Private member **R** views beyond the **B** floor (per **MD-08**, within **B** limits).

## 6. Reasoning (why this is the strongest UC choice)

1. **Coherence with “receive”** — Without some **B** trust statement, the Declaration sounds like maximal reception while **DR-10** stays empty.
2. **Avoids polemic** — Option B (limited corruption **B**) risks generalising “Jews of Medina” to Judaism today (**MD-05** cost). Wholesale **D2** contradicts **H** and Jewish/Christian membership.
3. **Honest about **H**** — Does not pretend variants do not exist.
4. **Muslim-readable** — Presents Qur’anic charges as **witness to misuse**, not as UC’s **H** verdict of total loss — leaves room for **R** diversity among Muslim members on *naṣṣ* vs *maʿnā* **if** they accept the **B** floor above.
5. **CCRP operational** — Gives leaders a default: **prefer readings that preserve moral coherence and witness function** rather than defaulting to “this layer lied.”

**Trade-off:** Strict textual-*taḥrīf* **R** Muslims and strict inerrantist Christians may decline UC or hold private dissent. **MD-08** allows recorded dissent on non-essential lines; **essential** register would need a new row if **B** is adopted (**DR-14** pattern).
