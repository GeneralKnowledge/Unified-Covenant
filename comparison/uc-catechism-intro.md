## God and the world

**Q. Who is God for Unified Covenant?**  
A. **One Sovereign** — creator, merciful and just, without rival. We reject *shirk*: giving ultimate loyalty to anything besides God.

**Q. Does UC teach the Trinity?**  
A. **Not as UC dogma.** We learn from Christian **R** tradition but do **not** require Nicene metaphysics for membership.

**Q. Why does the world exist?**  
A. So humanity may **know God**, **serve** in justice and mercy, and **care** for neighbours and creation.

## Scripture

**Q. What books does UC use?**  
A. **Tanakh, New Testament, and Qur’an** as **canonical witnesses**, read under **CCRP** when they seem to conflict.

**Q. What is “Sufficient Witness”?**  
A. God has preserved **enough continuity** in these texts that they remain **fit for covenant faith and moral instruction**. Variation and disagreement do **not** mean wholesale corruption; UC also does **not** teach perfect uniformity of every manuscript.

**Q. When scriptures disagree, what do we do?**  
A. We **classify** historical vs inherited vs binding claims, use context and the **preservation-first method**, seek moral coherence, and **record** what stays unresolved — we do not pretend all three traditions already agree.

## Jesus and Muhammad

**Q. Who is Jesus for UC?**  
A. **Jesus the Anointed** — uniquely **focal** teacher and servant; **crucified in history**; **vindicated by God** (**B**). Not the Nicene Second Person as UC requirement.

**Q. What about the cross and forgiveness?**  
A. The cross is **real suffering** and **witness** to God’s faithfulness; UC **does not** teach blood payment or penal substitution as **B**. We **repent**, **amend life**, and **repair** harm; God’s **mercy** is real. *How* forgiveness works in every detail (**UQ-03**) is **not** fully defined in UC **B**.

**Q. Who is Muhammad for UC?**  
A. **Prophet** and **seal of the UC canonical series** — the final **canonical** voice in our three-layer witness. He **does not** replace Jesus as centre of our story.

## Salvation and ethics

**Q. How does a person live rightly with God?**  
A. **Faith** in One Sovereign, **repentance**, **restitution** where possible, **mercy** toward others, and **covenant participation** — see [pastoral guide](./uc-pastoral-salvation-guide.md).

**Q. How do we decide hard moral questions?**  
A. **Justice and mercy paired**, truth, protection of the vulnerable, scripture via **CCRP**, and the five-step method in [ethics guide](./uc-ethics-practice-guide.md) — not a full legal code.

**Q. Must victims forgive abusers?**  
A. **No.** Forgiveness may be a **virtue** over time; **safety and justice** come first. God **judges** oppressors.

## Worship and time

**Q. How does UC worship together?**  
A. The **seven-part shared liturgy** (**B**) when we gather — [uc-shared-gathering-liturgy.md](./uc-shared-gathering-liturgy.md). You may join aloud, silently, or partially (**MD-08**).

**Q. When do we gather?**  
A. **Covenant Day** — normally **Sunday** (**B**), with local shift if Sunday is impracticable. Daily morning/evening prayer is **commended (**G**)**.

**Q. What are the main annual days?**  
A. **Covenant Renewal** (2nd Sunday of October, **B**); **Day of Turning** (Sunday before, **G**); **Day of Witness to the Afflicted** (2nd Sunday of March, **G**).

## Community

**Q. How do I become a member?**  
A. Assent to essential **B**, public **covenant entry**, commitment to **CCRP** outcomes — see [membership guide](./uc-membership-and-obligations.md). No comprehensive theology exam.

**Q. Is UC the only true religion?**  
A. UC is **one new religion** under One Sovereign. We **do not** claim to **replace** Judaism, Christianity, or Islam (**MD-05**).

**Q. May I also practice another faith?**  
A. Only where **conscience** and the other **R** authority allow; **conflicts must be acknowledged**, not hidden (**MD-08**).

---

## Hope

**Q. What happens after death?**  
A. **Judgment** is real; **hope of resurrection** in God’s mercy for the faithful repentant. UC does **not** bind detailed geography of heaven and hell as **B**.
