## The creed (spoken form)

*(Assembly or candidate may speak aloud, or listen in silence — **MD-08**.)*

**One Sovereign God — Merciful and Just — alone we worship, without partner or rival.**

**Jesus the Anointed is our centre: crucified in history, vindicated by God — not abandoned, not forgotten.**

**We receive Tanakh, New Testament, and Qur’an as trustworthy witnesses — sufficient for faith, instruction, and worship.**

**Muhammad we honour as prophet and seal of our canonical series — our doctrine, not a claim upon other religions.**

**We turn from wrongdoing, repair what we can, trust God’s mercy, and walk together in justice and mercy paired.**

**We are Unified Covenant — a new community alongside, not replacing, Judaism, Christianity, and Islam.**

**Amen.**

**One God; Jesus vindicated; three witnesses; Muhammad seal; repent and repair; justice with mercy; one new covenant people.**
