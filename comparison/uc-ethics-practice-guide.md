## 1. Principles (ordered for conflict, not for rank of importance)

| Principle | UC sense |
|-----------|----------|
| **Human dignity** | Persons are covenantally addressed — not disposable |
| **Truthfulness** | Honesty; no false witness |
| **Justice** | Fair dealing; proportion; protection of victims |
| **Mercy** | Remedy beyond bare minimum where wisdom allows |
| **Responsibility** | Answerability for choices and harm |
| **Restitution** | Repair to victims where possible |
| **Forgiveness** | Release and reconciliation **when appropriate** — not a weapon against victims |
| **Protection of vulnerable** | Priority when power is unequal |

**Pairing rule (**ADOPTED** direction):** Mercy **without** justice becomes excuse; justice **without** mercy becomes cruelty. UC seeks **both**.

---

## 2. Method — five steps for moral conflict

1. **Name the goods** — What values are in tension (e.g. mercy vs safety)?  
2. **Identify affected parties** — Especially vulnerable or harmed.  
3. **Consult witness** — Scripture via **CCRP**, adopted **B**, and **H** where relevant — **not** automatic import of halakha/sharīʿa/church law as **B**.  
4. **Test for cruelty and deceit** — Reject readings that bless oppression (**MD-07** moral coherence).  
5. **Decide, document, revisit** — Act; record dissent if **MD-08**; adjust if new facts emerge.

**When inherited rules conflict with contemporary circumstances:** distinguish **enduring principle** (e.g. do not steal) from **contingent application** (e.g. ancient war codes). Re-apply principle to present facts; **do not** silently discard scripture — **show** reasoning.

## 3. Standard tensions — default UC posture

| Tension | Default direction |
|---------|-------------------|
| **Mercy vs justice** | **Justice first** for safety and truth; **mercy** in penalty and long-term restoration where justice is satisfied |
| **Forgiveness requested, no restitution** | Forgiveness **from victims** not compelled; **penitent** must still **offer** repair; community **may** withhold trust |
| **Personal interest vs others’ welfare** | **Welfare of vulnerable** weighs heavier; self-care **not** at cost of serious harm to others |
| **Honesty vs harm** | No **blanket** “white lie” permission; **silence** or **refusal to speak** may beat coerced false witness |
| **Law vs conscience (**MD-08**)** | Record dissent; **do not** use conscience to excuse harm to others |

## 4. Worked examples (concise)

### Example A — Theft and repentance

**Case:** Member stole from neighbour; returns goods partially; neighbour bitter.

**UC path:** Full restitution where possible; written apology; accept legal consequences if applicable; **no** claim that prayer replaces return of goods. Leader **may** facilitate conversation; **may not** force neighbour’s forgiveness. Covenant Day confession aligns with **Day of Turning** practices.

### Example B — Domestic violence

**Case:** Spouse reports violence; perpetrator asks “mercy.”

**UC path:** **Safety and justice** first — separation, reporting, professional help. **Mercy** does **not** mean immediate reunion without change and accountability. Repentance = **cease**, **submit** to oversight, **long** amendment.

### Example C — Business pressure to deceive

**Case:** Employer demands false reporting; worker needs income.

**UC path:** **Truthfulness **B** ethic**; seek lawful alternatives; **MD-08** if resignation required — document conscience claim. Community **material care** where able (**PROPOSAL** mutual obligation).

### Example D — Inherited dietary or dress norm vs UC conscience

**Case:** Convert from Muslim **R** background; family expects sharīʿa norms; UC does not adopt sharīʿa as **B**.

**UC path:** **MD-08** — no comprehensive conformity test; member may keep **R** practice privately where conscience leads **if** not violating **B**. **MD-05** — no mockery of inherited **R**.

### Example E — Punishment vs restoration (community discipline)

**Case:** Leader abuses teaching role.

**UC path:** Remove from role; protect victims; **transparent** account; **justice** with path of **mercy** only after safety and demonstrated change — not secret cover-up.
