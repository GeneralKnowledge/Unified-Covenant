# Unified Covenant — Foundational practices

**Status:** **G** — official teaching and worship guidance (**MD-15**). **Binding liturgy text** remains [uc-shared-gathering-liturgy.md](./uc-shared-gathering-liturgy.md) (**B**, **MD-13**). This document **does not** replace or rewrite that script.

**Theology:** [unified-covenant-v0.7.md](./unified-covenant-v0.7.md) · Pastoral: [uc-pastoral-salvation-guide.md](./uc-pastoral-salvation-guide.md) · Ethics: [uc-ethics-practice-guide.md](./uc-ethics-practice-guide.md)

## 1. Daily prayer (**G**)

Use **morning** after waking and **evening** before sleep (or another fixed pair of times). Stand, sit, or kneel as you choose. You may speak aloud or silently.

### Morning

*(Individual or household)*

**Sovereign God — Merciful and Just — this day is Yours.**

**I turn to You alone, without partner or rival.**

**Grant me honesty in my work, courage against injustice, and a heart ready to serve neighbour and stranger.**

**If I have wronged anyone, turn me toward repair.**

**Guide my steps in the Unified Covenant. Amen.**

### Evening

**Sovereign God — Merciful and Just — I return this day to You.**

**I thank You for what sustained me; I confess what I have done and left undone.**

**Forgive what I cannot yet repair; strengthen what I must amend tomorrow.**

**Keep under Your care those who suffer, and those I love.**

**In trust I rest until the new day. Amen.**

*(Optional **O**:** brief reading from any canonical layer, one or two verses, under personal study — not a **B** lectionary requirement.)*

## 2. Covenant Day — order of worship (**G** wrapper around **B** liturgy)

**When:** **Sunday** normally (**B**, **MD-14**); another weekday **G** if Sunday is impracticable — teach Sunday as norm.

**Before gathering:** welcome; ensure reader and leader are assigned; prepare lectionary passage per [ccrp-case-record-md-13-liturgy.md](./ccrp-case-record-md-13-liturgy.md) or local **G** rotation (Tanakh / NT / Qur’an in balanced cycle).

### Order

| Step | Content | Class |
|------|---------|-------|
| 1 | **MD-05 disclaimer** (leader, one sentence): “We gather as Unified Covenant — not as synagogue, church, or mosque obligation.” | **G** |
| 2 | **Shared gathering liturgy** — full seven sections | **B** — [uc-shared-gathering-liturgy.md](./uc-shared-gathering-liturgy.md) |
| 3 | **Teaching** (10–25 min): scripture, ethics, or calendar season — **G** |
| 4 | **Announcements:** service projects, Day of Witness plans, mutual aid — **G** |
| 5 | **Dismissal** — liturgy §7 already closes; optional **O** meal or fellowship |

**Rest:** Where practicable, members abstain from **non-essential commerce and labour** on Covenant Day — **G** rest ethic, not a universal employment law.

**Leader guide:** [uc-shared-gathering-liturgy-leader-guide.md](./uc-shared-gathering-liturgy-leader-guide.md)

## 3. Covenant Renewal Day — complete service (**B** day; **G** order except **B** liturgy)

**When:** Second **Sunday of October** (**B**, **MD-15**).

**Purpose:** Reaffirm *brit*; welcome new members; read Declaration; renew commitment.

### Order

1. **Gathering hymn or silence** (3 min) — **G**; no prescribed text  
2. **Opening word** (leader): purpose of Renewal — **G**  
3. **Shared gathering liturgy** — **B** (full)  
4. **Declaration of faith** — leader reads [v0.4.2 Declaration](./unified-covenant-v0.4.2.md) or [v0.7 summary](./unified-covenant-v0.7.md); assembly **may** respond **“We affirm this covenant”** — **G** (not a new **B** formula)  
5. **Covenant entry / reaffirmation**  
   - *New members:* public assent to essential **B**; sign or verbal witness per local **G** record  
   - *Existing members:* **“I renew my commitment to One Sovereign, this canon, and this community.”** — **G**  
6. **Teaching:** Sufficient Witness, Covenant Day, mutual obligation (15 min) — **G**  
7. **Intercession:** community, nations, afflicted — **G**  
8. **Communal meal** — **O** strongly encouraged  
9. **Closing:** liturgy §7 blessing if not already used; or brief **G** blessing repeating Merciful and Just send-off

## 4. Day of Turning — guide (**G**)

**When:** Sunday **immediately before** Covenant Renewal Day.

**Focus:** Repentance, restitution planning, charity.

### At home (individual/family)

1. **Quiet** (10 min)  
2. **Examine:** “Whom have I harmed? What duty did I neglect?” — write if helpful  
3. **Morning or evening daily prayer** (§1) with extended confession  
4. **One concrete act:** apology, payment, restored contact, or service — **same day if possible**

### With community (recommended **G**)

1. Short gathering (30–45 min)  
2. Liturgy §§1–3 only (**B** portions) — optional skip witness reading  
3. **Leader:** invite silent confession (5 min)  
4. **Read** [uc-pastoral-salvation-guide.md](./uc-pastoral-salvation-guide.md) §4.1–4.2 summary — **G**  
5. **Charity collection** or name local recipients — **G**  
6. **Commitment:** each person names **one** amendment for the year — **O** shared aloud

## 5. Day of Witness to the Afflicted — service and action (**G**)

**When:** Second **Sunday of March**.

**Focus:** Solidarity, justice, practical service (**DR-12**).

### Service (45–60 min)

1. Opening: “Today we witness to those who suffer injustice, illness, or poverty.” — **G**  
2. **Liturgy** §§1–2, §6 (justice commitment) — **B** where marked  
3. **Witness reading:** choose passage on oppression, exile, or care for poor (CCRP record) — **B** slot + **G** choice  
4. **Homily:** **H** passion / **B** vindication **hope only** — **no** new atonement **B** (**MD-12**)  
5. **Intercession** for named local needs — **G**  
6. Closing blessing from liturgy §7 — **B**  

### Community activity (same day or following week)

- Visit sick or isolated members  
- Donate time or goods to a vetted aid organisation  
- **Document** project briefly for community memory — **G**

## 6. Neighbor-study days (**O**)

When Jewish, Christian, or Muslim neighbors observe **R** festivals, a UC group **may** meet for **comparative study** — explicitly **not** UC holy observance.

**Frame (leader):** “We learn; we do not claim to observe Passover, Easter, Ramaḍān, or High Holy Days as UC **B**.”

**Format:** reading from matrix or chronicle; respectful discussion; no mockery (**MD-05**).

## 7. Life rites (**G**)

### 7.1 Naming (birth or adoption)

**Setting:** home or small gathering.

**Leader/parent:**

**Sovereign God, Merciful and Just, we thank You for this child [name].**

**May [name] grow in knowledge of You, in justice and mercy, and in this covenant community.**

**May we who surround this life teach faithfully and protect tenderly. Amen.**

*(Optional **O**:** community welcomes child; no **B** requirement for godparents.)*

### 7.2 Covenant entry (adult)

**Prerequisites:** instruction on essential **B**; assent; **MD-08** dissent process explained for non-essentials.

**Public act:**

1. Declaration read (see Renewal Day §4)  
2. Candidate: **“I enter the Unified Covenant before God and this assembly.”**  
3. Community: **“We witness your covenant and welcome you among us.”** — **G**  
4. Record name/date in community register — **G**  

### 7.3 Marriage

**Core vows (**G**):**

**Before One Sovereign and this community, we covenant in fidelity, mercy, and truth.**

**We will bear with one another in weakness, speak honestly, refuse cruelty, and seek repair when we fail.**

**May our household serve justice and welcome the stranger. Amen.**

*(Optional: include full shared liturgy §§1–3 — **B** portions.)*

### 7.4 Serious illness

**Visiting prayer (leader or friend):**

**Sovereign God, Merciful and Just, You know the pain of [name].**

**Grant comfort, skilled care, and honest hope.**

**If recovery comes, turn it to gratitude and service; if death approaches, grant peace and trust in Your judgment and mercy. Amen.**

**Community:** practical support (meals, transport, advocacy) — **G** duty.

### 7.5 Death and mourning

**At deathbed or memorial:**

**Sovereign God, into Your hands we entrust [name].**

**You are Merciful and Just; we trust Your judgment and the hope of resurrection You have placed before us.**

**Comfort those who grieve; bind us together in truth and care. Amen.**

**Mourning period:** community checks on bereaved; memorial may use full shared liturgy — **B** text.

## 8. Leader guidance — hard pastoral cases (**G**)

Summaries; full detail in pastoral and ethics guides.

| Situation | Direction |
|-----------|-----------|
| **Grief** | Presence first; avoid clichés; liturgy and resurrection hope; professional grief support **G** |
| **Doubt** | Distinguish honest seeking from defiance; **MD-08**; practice before full intellectual assent |
| **Serious wrongdoing** | Stop harm; legal duty; restitution; no “cheap forgiveness” narrative |
| **Victims of abuse** | Safety first; believe report; remove offender from roles; **do not** pressure reconciliation |
| **Incomplete repentance** | Persist in repair; divine mercy for shortfall with amended life; victims not compelled to trust |

**Escalation:** document; involve second leader; maintainer/panel if **B** dispute — per **CCRP**.
