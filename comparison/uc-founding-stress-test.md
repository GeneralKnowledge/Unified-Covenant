# Part B — Moral dilemmas

### B1. Mercy vs justice (sentencing a remorseful offender)

**UC:** **Justice first** — truth, protection, proportion; **mercy** in penalty and restoration **after** safety. See ethics guide Example E.

### B2. Forgiveness requested, no restitution

**UC:** Divine mercy **does not** cancel **duty to repair**; victims **not** compelled to reconcile. Penitent **offers** what they can.

### B3. Whistleblowing vs loyalty

**UC:** **Truthfulness** and **protection of vulnerable** outweigh private loyalty; document conscience (**MD-08**) if employment conflicts.

### B4. Lying to save life (hide the persecuted)

**UC:** **No** blanket “white lie” **B**; **silence** or **refusal to betray** preferred over false witness; **H** cases → CCRP if community dispute.

### B5. Wealth and inequality

**UC:** **Generosity** as worship; fair dealing; **not** a detailed economic **B** — **G** ethics + **DR-12** vulnerable-first.

### B6. Divorce and broken households

**UC:** **Fidelity and mercy** in marriage vows (**G** rite); when breakdown occurs — **honesty**, **protect children**, **repair** financial/abuse harm; no UC **B** canon law on remarriage — **C** or **G** local pastoral until **§12** rule.

### B7. Military service and violence

**UC:** **Not** a pacifist **B** unless adopted later; **refuse oppression in our name**; conscientious objection **MD-08** path; lethal force **G** pastoral — minimum harm, accountability.

# Part C — Ordinary life

### C1. Must work on Covenant Day

**UC:** **Sunday norm **B****; **G** shift for assembly + **G** rest ethic. Work when **necessary** is not apostasy; **habitual** neglect of community is pastoral, not automatic expulsion.

### C2. Mixed-faith household

**UC:** **MD-05** + **MD-08** — honesty with children; no mockery of **R** parent’s faith; UC member **does not** require spouse’s conversion.

### C3. Newcomer who struggles with Muhammad or crucifixion

**UC:** **MD-08** — doubt ≠ defiance; assent to **essential **B**** at entry; teaching over time; **cannot** deny **DR-03**/**DR-04**/**DR-08**/**DR-10** as member.

### C4. Only three members in a city

**UC:** Covenant Day on **G** weekday if needed; liturgy **B** when two or more gather; maintainer contact for CCRP.

### C5. Chronic illness — cannot attend

**UC:** **Not** a faith failure; community **visits** (practices §7.4); liturgy at home **optional **O**** read silently.

### C6. Member repeats gossip, never violence

**UC:** **Repentance** + amends to reputation; **Day of Turning** discipline; discipline **G** — exclusion only if refusal to amend and harm continues.

### C7. Reading Qur’an and NT same week — contradictory moral examples

**UC:** **CCRP** + ethics five-step; **Sufficient Witness** — both layers instruct under procedure, not “pick one book.”
