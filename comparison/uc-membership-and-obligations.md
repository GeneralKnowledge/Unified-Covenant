# Unified Covenant — Membership, worship, and obligations

**Status:** **G** — official newcomer and leader guide (**MD-15**). **Binding essentials** remain [v0.4.2 §14](./unified-covenant-v0.4.2.md).

**Related:** [uc-creed.md](./uc-creed.md) · [uc-catechism-intro.md](./uc-catechism-intro.md) · [uc-foundational-practices.md](./uc-foundational-practices.md)

---

## 1. Who membership is for

Unified Covenant is for anyone who **freely assents** to UC **essential doctrine**, enters the **covenant publicly**, and commits to live under **CCRP** and mutual obligation — without pretending to be Judaism, Christianity, or Islam.

**Not required:** ethnic background; perfect attendance; vocal repetition of every liturgy line; agreement with every **G** teaching memo; resolution of **UQ-03** atonement mechanics.

---

## 2. What you assent to (essential **B** / **H**)

At entry you affirm the [creed](./uc-creed.md) in substance and the register in **v0.4.2 §14**, including:

| You affirm | Plain meaning |
|------------|----------------|
| **DR-01** | One Sovereign God; no *shirk* |
| **DR-02** | Jesus the Anointed (UC definition) |
| **DR-03** | Historical crucifixion under Pilate |
| **DR-04** | Divine vindication of the Anointed |
| **DR-06** | Three-layer Unified Canon as witnesses |
| **DR-07** | CCRP when scripture conflicts |
| **DR-08** | Muhammad — seal of **UC** canonical series |
| **DR-09** | Non-supersession of Judaism, Christianity, Islam |
| **DR-10** | Sufficient Witness — trustworthy canon |
| **DR-12** | Accountability, justice–mercy, care for vulnerable |
| **DR-15** | Covenant Day — Sunday norm |
| **DR-16** | Covenant Renewal Day — 2nd Sun Oct |
| **MD-13** | Shared gathering liturgy form when UC worships |

**You do not affirm as UC dogma:** Nicene Trinity (**DR-11** is **R** reference only); penal substitution; wholesale *taḥrīf* polemic; inherited halakha/sharīʿa as automatic UC law.

**Recorded dissent (**MD-08**):** Non-essential **G** or interpretive points may be **registered** without losing membership if **B** is not denied.

## 3. How to enter

1. **Learn** — [catechism](./uc-catechism-intro.md), [v0.7](./unified-covenant-v0.7.md) (or leader summary).  
2. **Prepare** — name conflicts with other **R** loyalties honestly.  
3. **Gather** — community witnesses (or maintainer-approved process in small/dispersed groups).  
4. **Assent** — creed + Declaration substance.  
5. **Record** — name, date, community register (**G**).  

Rite text: [uc-foundational-practices.md §7.2](./uc-foundational-practices.md).

**Children:** raised in the story; **adult assent** for full membership.

---

## 4. Worship — what is expected

| Rhythm | Expectation | Class |
|--------|-------------|-------|
| **Covenant Day** | **Primary** communal assembly + liturgy when a community exists | **B** day; **G** if dispersed |
| **Shared liturgy** | **B** text when UC gathers — participation modes flexible (**MD-08**) | **B** |
| **Daily prayer** | Commended | **G** |
| **Covenant Renewal** | Annual reaffirmation | **B** |
| **Day of Turning / Witness** | Strongly commended | **G** |

**Not a membership test:** missing a Sunday; silent participation; illness; essential work — though **habitual absence** from all community may be a pastoral conversation, not automatic expulsion.

**Order of worship:** [uc-foundational-practices.md §2](./uc-foundational-practices.md).

## 5. Member obligations (mutual **G** / **B** ethics)

| Obligation | Source |
|------------|--------|
| **Worship One Sovereign** without *shirk* | **B** |
| **Speak truthfully** about UC to outsiders | **MD-08** |
| **Protect the vulnerable**; refuse cruelty in UC’s name | **DR-12** |
| **Repent and repair** when you harm others | Pastoral **G** + **B** ethic |
| **Support community** materially where able | Mutual **G** |
| **Use CCRP** in disputes over scripture or doctrine | **DR-07** |
| **Report abuse**; cooperate with safety measures | Ethics **G** |

**Leaders additionally:** use **B** liturgy; **MD-05** disclaimers on Covenant Day; no coercion; remove harm-doers from teaching roles.

---

## 6. Leaving and dual identity

**Departure:** voluntary; no theological hostage-taking.

**Dual identity:** permitted only where conscience and external **R** authority allow; **do not** misrepresent UC as identical to another religion.

- [ ] Morning or evening prayer (**G**)  
- [ ] One act of justice or mercy (**B** ethic)  
- [ ] Covenant Day assembly when possible  
- [ ] If wronged someone — one step toward repair
