## 2. Coherent UC account (recommended teaching shape)

**Salvation** — being **right with God** and **oriented toward vindicated life** — is **gift and demand** together:

1. **Faith** — trust in One Sovereign; refusal of *shirk* and self-worship.  
2. **Repentance** — turning, confession, amended conduct.  
3. **Repair** — restitution to victims where possible; humility where not.  
4. **Mercy** — received from God; extended to others without canceling justice.  
5. **Covenant participation** — shared worship, mutual obligation (**MD-08**: no line-by-line belief test).

The **cross** is **witness** to innocent suffering, faithfulness under affliction, and **God’s vindication pattern** — **not** a **B** explanation that God required blood payment to forgive.

**Judgment** is real; **hope** is real. UC expects accountability beyond death and **hope of resurrection** in mercy for the faithful repentant — without binding detailed geography of hell/heaven as **B**.

---

## 3. How the pieces relate

```text
God (mercy + justice, one character)
  → addresses humans in covenant
  → revelation witnesses to duty and hope
  → wrongdoing breaks trust and harms neighbours
  → repentance + repair + divine mercy
  → vindication hope (Jesus as prototype witness, MD-12)
  → ethical life continues in gratitude, not merit-hoarding
```

**Jesus’ crucifixion and vindication** teach that **God sees** affliction and **does not** treat faithful suffering as the last word. **Muhammad’s witness** reinforces **accountability** and **closure of the canonical series** — not a second saviour.

## 4. Pastoral situations

### 4.1 Serious wrongdoing (violence, betrayal, sustained deceit)

**Teach:**

- Wrong is **real**; minimising harm is not repentance.  
- **Stop** the wrong; **confess** to God; **seek** to warn/protect future victims.  
- **Restitution** and **accountability** to those harmed where possible — including legal consequences where required for justice.  
- **God’s mercy** is available to the **genuinely turning** heart — but UC **does not** promise absence of earthly consequences or instant trust from victims.  
- Community **may** impose discipline (**G** procedures) protecting the vulnerable.

**Do not teach:** “Belief alone erases harm”; “Cross paid so victims must forgive instantly.”

### 4.2 Repentance without full repair

**Teach:**

- **Incomplete repair** does not excuse abandoning effort. Offer **what can** be done: apology, partial restitution, changed role, long-term humility.  
- **Victims** are not obliged to reconcile or trust on demand (**MD-08** conscience).  
- **Divine mercy** covers **shortfall** where the penitent **persists** in amended life — **without** UC specifying **mechanism** (**UQ-03** **C**).  
- Prayer: honesty about limits; ask for strength to repair more if opportunity opens.

### 4.3 Person harmed by another

**Teach:**

- **Anger** and **grief** are not sins; **vengeance** belongs to God and lawful justice, not private cruelty.  
- **Forgiveness** of the offender (when offered) is **virtue**, not **duty** on a timetable; **release** from bitterness may be a **long** path.  
- Seek **safety**, **legal protection**, **community support**.  
- God **judges** oppressors; vindication hope includes **moral** universe not indifferent to pain.

### 4.4 Struggle with belief

**Teach:**

- **Honest doubt** is not defiance if the heart seeks truth.  
- **MD-08:** membership does not require exhaustive theological certainty.  
- **Practice** — prayer, ethical action, attendance — may **precede** full intellectual assent.  
- **Defiance** (knowing rejection of One Sovereign while claiming UC identity) is pastoral crisis — not the same as doubt.
