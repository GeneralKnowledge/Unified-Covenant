## Design principles (why this is UC’s calendar)

1. **One rhythm, one story** — Time structures **covenant identity**, not imitation of Jewish, Christian, or Islamic **R** obligation calendars (**MD-05**).
2. **Vindication and renewal** — Weekly and annual days echo **repentance → witness → hope** aligned with **MD-12** (vindication without substitution **B**).
3. **Minimal but lived** — Few **B** anchors; **G** enrichment; **O** optional study — avoid calendar crowding.
4. **Practical dispersion** — Named global rhythm with **G** local shift where Sunday assembly is impossible.

## Weekly: Covenant Day (**MD-14** recommendation)

### Preferred **B** rule

UC observes **Covenant Day** each **Sunday** as the **primary day of assembly and rest**: communal **shared liturgy**, teaching, mutual obligation, and — where conscience and livelihood allow — abstention from non-essential commerce and labour.

### Why Sunday (UC-native reasoning, not “Christian calendar adoption”)

- UC centres **God’s vindication of the Anointed** after innocent suffering (**MD-12**). A weekly return to **gathered hope** fits **vindication memory**, not Nicene liturgy.
- **Global coordination** — one named weekday aids dispersed identity without requiring every locale to invent its own **B** day.
- **Practical** — widespread rest patterns in many regions; liturgy **B** whenever the community gathers (**MD-13**) gains a default anchor.

### **MD-05** disclaimers (required in teaching if **B** adopted)

- Covenant Day is **not** “Christian Sunday obligation” or “Muslim Friday” or “Jewish Shabbat.”
- UC **does not** claim inherited calendars are invalid; members may still observe **R** days in source communities where conscience allows (**MD-08**).

### **G** local shift

Communities **may** hold primary assembly another weekday when Sunday is impossible (essential work, persecution, small dispersed groups), but **teach** Sunday as the **normative** Covenant Day for UC worldwide.

### Trade-off

- **Cost:** Christian-majority optics (“new church”); Muslim members may prefer Friday association.
- **Benefit:** Real weekly sacred rhythm; clearer religion-in-time than pure **G** scheduling.

**Alternative (maintainer):** Retain Phase 7.7 preference — **no universal **B** weekly day**, **G** local only — see [decision sheet](./phase-8-2-maintainer-decision-sheet.md).

## Optional: Neighbor-study days (**O**)

When Jewish, Christian, or Muslim neighbors observe **R** festivals, UC communities **may** schedule **study** (comparative, respectful) — **explicit framing:** study, not UC observance. No **B** obligation.
