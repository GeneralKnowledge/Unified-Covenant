## 1 — Opening and gathering

*(Leader)*

**We gather as the Unified Covenant assembly — a new community under One Sovereign, alongside—not in place of—Jewish, Christian, and Muslim communities and their own authorities.**

*(Optional: name of locality, occasion, or visitors — **O**, brief.)*

*(All)*

**We enter this time in reverence, ready to listen, speak honestly, and act justly.**

---

## 2 — Address to One Sovereign God

*(All)*

**Sovereign God — Merciful and Just — we turn to You alone, without partner or rival.**

**You are the source of life, the judge of nations, and the hope of the faithful.**

**We do not claim that every path names You as we do; we claim this covenant names You as our One Sovereign.**

---

## 3 — Shared prayer: mercy, gratitude, repentance, renewal

*(Leader, then all repeat each paragraph, or all read together.)*

**Merciful One, receive our thanks for daily bread, for companions, and for the witnesses of scripture that guide us.**

**We confess before You what we have done and left undone — harsh words, neglected duty, indifference to the vulnerable.**

**Grant us repentance that changes how we live, not words alone.**

**Renew in us courage to follow Jesus the Anointed in trust and service — crucified in history, vindicated by You, held in our story without Nicene dogma or a new salvation mechanism.**

**Seal our hearts in loyalty to Muhammad as prophet and seal of our canonical series — our UC doctrine, not a claim that others must adopt our formula.**

---

## 4 — Canonical witness reading

*(Leader)*

**We now hear a passage from the Unified Canon as canonical witness — interpreted under our procedure, not as proof that every tradition agrees with our reading.**

*(Reader)*

`[ One passage from Tanakh, New Testament, or Qur’an, chosen under the published G lectionary and any required CCRP record. Before reading, the leader names the layer and translation. ]`

*(Leader — after reading)*

**May this witness instruct our conscience; may we not use it to settle what our constitution leaves open — including scriptural preservation, disputed interpretation of the passion, or detailed atonement mechanics.**

---

## 5 — Silence or reflection

*(All)*

*(Two minutes of silence, or a longer period on solemn days — **G** timing.)*

*(Optional **O**: quiet shared phrase agreed locally, if it does not add new **B** claims.)*

---

## 6 — Shared commitment to justice, mercy, and faithful conduct

*(All)*

**We commit ourselves again to justice and mercy paired — not as slogans, but as deeds:**

**— to protect the vulnerable and speak truth without cruelty;**

**— to refuse oppression in our name;**

**— to carry this covenant into our households, work, and public life.**

*(Optional **O**: brief naming of a local service project or need.)*

---

## 7 — Closing prayer and blessing

*(Leader)*

**Sovereign God, send us from this gathering as faithful members of the Unified Covenant — not as replacements for synagogue, church, or mosque, but as bearers of this *brit* in the world.**

*(All)*

**May the Merciful and Just guide our steps until we meet again. Amen.**

*(Leader)*

**The gathering is ended. Go in peace and in active mercy.**
