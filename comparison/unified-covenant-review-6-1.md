# Unified Covenant — Phase 6.1 Adversarial Review of v0.1

**Status:** Critical review only. Does **not** modify [unified-covenant-v0.1.md](./unified-covenant-v0.1.md) or begin v0.2.

**Subject:** [Unified Covenant v0.1](./unified-covenant-v0.1.md) (merged via PR #11, `96b4b86` on `main`).

**Evidence base:** [history/01](../history/01-before-the-branches.md)–[04](../history/04-the-emergence-of-islam.md); [evidence-matrix.md](./evidence-matrix.md); [convergence-assessment.md](./convergence-assessment.md); [convergence-source-audit.md](./convergence-source-audit.md); [sources/registry.md](../sources/registry.md).

**Method note:** Perspectives A–C are **analytical lenses**, not spokespeople for Judaism, Christianity, or Islam. Internal diversity within each tradition is assumed where the chronicle and comparison README require it.

---

## Executive summary

v0.1 is **honest about many Level C incompatibilities** and correctly demotes several essay moves (archetype table, law/grace triad) that the convergence assessment marks translation-dependent. Its **central structural defect** is **classification slippage**: several **project-level** or **methodological** commitments are labelled **Level A (common ground)**, especially **UC-06** (historiography) and **UC-07** (constitutional bracketing of soteriology). That slippage makes the constitution read like **interfaith agreement** when the repository supports only **qualified motif overlap** or **historian-only** convergence.

The proposal is **most coherent** as a **philosophical synthesis with a thin coexistence pact** (identity 2, partially identity 1)—not as evidence that the three religions share one theology. **Legitimate theological disagreements** (Trinity, 4:157, final revelation) are **recorded**, not solved; several **design defects** (epistemic lanes without priority rules, membership under-specified, internal mis-tagging of UC-08 in Part III) would **undermine practice** if v0.1 were treated as a living constitution without revision.

### UC-05, UC-06, UC-07 (focused)

**UC-05 — Prophetic moral critique**

- **Support:** Matrix § F; convergence §3 **Qualified** on **shared memory** and **moral critique** motifs (Ch.1–4 prophetic rhetoric).
- **Weakness:** **Finality** and **Jesus/Muhammad** roles **disqualify** unqualified “shared prophetic office” language; v0.1 qualifications exist but **principle title** suggests **more unity** than chronicle supports.
- **Verdict:** **Partly** defensible at Level A **only** with **narrower** wording (e.g. **Hebrew Bible prophetic idiom** + **recognition that later traditions reinterpret**).

**UC-06 — Historical baseline on Jesus**

- **Support:** Ch.3 § C; `jesus-crucifixion-pilate`; matrix disagreement §2 layer A; convergence §9a.
- **Critical distinction:** **Historians** (including many Jews and Muslims doing history) **accept** crucifixion; **Religious tradition** of **normative Islam** **denies** killing/crucifixion **theologically**. These are **different claim types**—v0.1 **separates lanes** but labels principle **Level A — common-ground commitment**, which **conflates** categories.
- **Recommendation:** **Split** into (1) **project historical baseline** (Level B or new category **H — historical constraint**); (2) optional **theological** statements **per tradition** (Level C / outside pact).

**UC-07 — Eschatological humility**

- **Support:** Convergence §7 **Qualified** on accountability + **limits of human knowledge** about afterlife; essay VI.
- **Critical:** **Bracketing** soteriology is **v0.1’s constitutional choice**, **not** something all three **affirm** as a **shared creed**. Christians affirm **detailed** resurrection faith; Muslims **detailed** judgment/Qur’anic eschatology. **“Humility”** is **philosophical packaging** of **non-adjudication**.
- **Verdict:** **Misclassified Level A** → should be **Level B** (constructive) or **split** (motif Level A / bracketing Level B).

**Supernatural claims:** Ch.3 § D and matrix § I: historians **document belief**, do not verify. v0.1 **respects** this in UC-08; **does not** use history to **refute** 4:157 **theologically**—good. **Defect** is **labelling**, not **automatic rejection** of revelation.

## Perspective F — Internal constitutional critic

### Unified worldview or list?

The twelve principles **cluster** into: **(1)** thin theology (UC-01), **(2)** ethics motifs (UC-02–UC-04), **(3)** history/method (UC-05–UC-08), **(4)** political-epistemic norms (UC-09–UC-12). **Coherence** comes from **Part II purpose** (inquiry pact), **not** from a **single metaphysical arch**. **Acceptable** for a **draft**; **weak** as **“recognisable worldview”** (v0.1 preamble claim)—**conceptual overstretch**.

| Pair | Tension |
|------|---------|
| UC-06 vs. UC-C02 / Limits of authority | Historical lane **mandatory** for project; theological lane **protected**—**participants who reject historical lane** (some Muslim **Religious tradition** readings) **cannot** participate **without split identity**. |

### Membership

Part II: **working pact**, not conversion; **dissent on Level B expected**. **Unclear:** Must a participant **affirm UC-06** historically? **Must they silence** 4:157 in **project forums**? **Undetermined**—**design defect**.

### Revision mechanism

**Records** disagreements; **does not decide** when **neutrality impossible** (UC-C items). **Adequate** for **research repository**; **inadequate** for **binding constitution** without **arbitration rule** (author decision required).

**Scenario 3 — UC-11 vs. binding fatwa / halakhic ruling**

## Test 2 — Truth and authority problem (worked example)

**Example**

1. **Historical (well supported):** Jesus crucified under Pilate (`jesus-crucifixion-pilate`, Ch.3, matrix disagreement §2 layer A).
2. **Participant M (Muslim, normative reading):** Q. 4:157 (`quran-4-157-crucifixion`, Ch.4) — foes did not kill/crucify him; **Qur’an** decisive.
3. **Participant C (Christian):** Resurrection **Religious tradition**; Gospels **decisive** for **theological** truth.
4. **Constitution must:** adopt, reject, or leave open.

**v0.1 answer:** **UC-06** adopts (1) for **historical lane**; (2)(3) in **theological lane** + UC-C02 **open**; **Limits of authority** forbid overriding (1) in **historical lane**.

**Is fourfold discipline a **decision procedure**?** **No.** It **sorts** claims; **default priority** (**history in public lane**) is **implicit**, **not argued**. **Missing rule (design defect):** Explicit **scope rule**—e.g. “Historical conclusions bind **only** collaborative historical appendices, not **confessional** speech acts of participants”—or **require** **opt-in** to historical baseline.

**Categorising does not resolve disagreement** when **one text** must be **official project statement**.
