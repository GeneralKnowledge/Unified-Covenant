## §1 — Identity and purpose

Unified Covenant (UC) is a **new religious community** attempting a **genuinely unified** Abrahamic faith: one coherent worship, ethics, canon discipline, and covenant identity **drawn critically** from Jewish, Christian, and Islamic inheritance—without claiming those religions were secretly identical or that UC **fulfils**, **supersedes**, or **replaces** them (**MD-05**).

**Why it exists:** Shared historical contact and scriptural family (matrix **Shared inheritance**) make **honest reconciliation** possible in places—and **irreconciliable conflict** in others. UC **chooses** where it must, **preserves inherited revelation wherever this can be defended** through careful textual, historical, linguistic, theological, and interpretive analysis (**MD-07**), and records costs.

**MD-07 clarifications:** “**Defensible**” follows the **preservation-first method** (§2.1)—it **does not** promise that every **R** claim can be reconciled. **MD-06** / **DR-10** **Sufficient Witness **B**** (Phase 8.3) settles UC **preservation theology** at membership level; preserving a tradition’s **strongest defensible** reading **≠** accepting **every** interpretation associated with that tradition.

**What it unites:** Not three identical theologies, but **one community** under **One Sovereign**, the **Unified Canon** (§5), **Jesus the Anointed** (§4), **Muhammad as seal of the Abrahamic canonical series** (§6), shared **ethical covenant** (§8), and **CCRP** interpretive procedure (§5.3) (**MD-03**).

UC is **not** Judaism, Christianity, or Islam. Jewish, Christian, and Muslim communities **continue** with their own **R** authorities intact outside UC (**MD-05**).

## §2 — Epistemic and interpretive principles

### 2.1 Preservation-first sequence

When texts or traditions appear to contradict:

1. Identify the **exact** conflict (same event? same sense of “God”? same authority claim?).
2. Examine **language**, genre, context, translation, and **internal diversity** in each stream.
3. State the **strongest defensible** version of each **R** position.
4. Test whether the conflict is **real**, **apparent**, or **category error** (H vs R vs B).
5. If irreconciliable in the same sense, **record** it—do not relabel as harmony.
6. Adopt **B** only with explicit **cost**; never as faux **A** consensus.

### 2.2 Classification (A / B / H / C)

| Label | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| **A** | Bounded **motif** convergence (qualified; not identical grounds) |
| **B** | **UC doctrine** — binds members |
| **H** | **Historical baseline** for UC teaching about past events/transmission |
| **C** | **Contested / open** — no definitive UC ruling yet |

One topic may need **split entries** (e.g. crucifixion **H**, passion theology **C** between **R** streams, vindication **B**).

### 2.3 Authority kinds

| Kind | Role |
|------|------|
| **H** | Historical inquiry (`jesus-crucifixion-pilate`, manuscript facts) |
| **R** | Inherited **Religious tradition** claims (Nicene creed, *tawḥīd*, oral Torah, Q. 4:157 as read by communities) |
| **B** | UC membership doctrine (this document) |
| **Procedural** | CCRP (§5.3) — **not** divine revelation |

**H does not cancel R** in their own communities; **R does not erase H** in UC history class. **B** decides UC worship and membership.

## §3 — God and divine unity (**MD-01**)

### 3.1 UC affirmation (**B**)

UC affirms **One Sovereign God** (*YHWH* / *Allāh* in inherited speech): **creator** of heaven and earth, **without partner or rival** (*shirk* rejected), **actively merciful and just**, who **speaks and acts in history** through covenant and prophecy.

God’s **unity of purpose and character** is affirmed. UC **does not** teach that God is a committee, a pantheon, or an undifferentiated philosophical abstraction indifferent to Israel, the Church, or the *umma*.

### 3.2 What UC does **not** affirm

| Stream | Normative claim UC **declines as UC dogma** |
|--------|---------------------------------------------|
| **Nicene Christianity** | Son **homoousios** with Father; incarnate Second Person of Trinity as **binding UC metaphysics** (`nicene-creed-325`) |
| **Judaism** | UC **does not** replace **brit**-particular YHWH–Israel covenant with generic monotheism |
| **Islam** | UC **does not** reduce *tawḥīd* to slogan while ignoring Qur’anic **anti-sonship** polemic |

UC **does not** claim **equivalence** to Nicene Trinity, rabbinic **YHWH–Israel** exclusivity, or full *tawḥīd* **R** formulation—while **learning from** each.

### 3.3 Interpretive posture toward scripture

- **Tanakh:** God’s **covenant name** and **exclusive devotion** to YHWH (motif **A** at high level; **brit** theology remains **R** Jewish).
- **NT:** Father-language and **exalted Christ** devotion (Ch.3; Hurtado) read as **witness** to God’s action through Jesus—not as automatic Nicene ontology.
- **Qur’an:** *Tawḥīd* and rejection of divine sonship (Ch.4) **inform** UC anti-*shirk* **B**; UC does **not** treat ʿĪsā as divine Son in Nicene sense.

**Ontological Nicene question:** UC **does not** resolve consubstantiality via “modes of action” language (**MD-01**). Economic descriptions of God’s work **do not** settle what Nicaea addressed.

### 3.4 Costs

- **Nicene Christians:** UC is **another religion**, not incomplete Christianity.
- **Muslims:** UC retains **Jesus** as central—**not** standard *tawḥīd*-only Christology.
- **Jews:** UC uses **messianic** language for Jesus—**not** rabbinic messianism.

## §4 — Jesus the Anointed (**MD-01**, **MD-02**)

### 4.1 Definition: **Jesus the Anointed** (**B**)

In UC, **Jesus the Anointed** (*ha-Māshíaḥ* / *al-Masīḥ*) means:

> God’s **appointed and uniquely focal** agent in the Abrahamic covenant story, through whom **mercy, teaching, and covenant renewal** are **especially disclosed** to UC; crucified under Roman authority (**H**); **not** abandoned by God; **vindicated and exalted** by God (**B** — see §4.4).

**Not implied:** Nicene **eternal Son**; **mere** prophet interchangeable with others; Qur’anic denial of **all** crucifixion events without qualification of **H**.

### 4.2 Messiahship in UC

- **Positive:** “Anointed” = **commissioned** by God for **turning hearts**, **embodying mercy**, **bearing witness** to One Sovereign, and **occupying the centre of UC retelling** of salvation history.
- **Distinct from Jewish normative messiah:** UC **does not** claim rabbinic messianic fulfilment or Torah-abrogating king (**R** Jewish rejection documented — `jewish-rejection-jesus-messiah-normative`).
- **Distinct from Islamic ʿĪsā:** UC affirms **historical crucifixion (H)** and **cross as real suffering**; honours Qur’anic **Messenger/Messiah** titles without Nicene incarnation.

### 4.3 Crucifixion — historical (**H**, **MD-02**)

UC **teaches as history:** Jesus of Nazareth, a Jewish figure, was **executed by crucifixion** under Roman authority (Pontius Pilate) — **Well established** (`jesus-crucifixion-pilate`; Ch.3 § C).

**Separate question:** Theological **meaning** of that death (**R** / **B**) — not settled by **H** alone.

### 4.4 Dominant Muslim reading of Q. 4:157 (**R**, not UC)

**Arabic (core):** *wa mā qatalūhu wa mā ṣalabūhu wa lākin shubbiha lahum* (Q 4:157).

**Dominant tafsīr trajectory** (classical and modern majority surveyed in [4:157 research](../research/phase-6-3-quran-4-157.md); Mevorach 2023 argues strong linguistic case): **Opponents did not kill or crucify Jesus** — often extended to **no crucifixion of Jesus**. **Substitution** readings supply narratives **not explicit in the verse** (Ṭabarī reports; research module).

**Internal diversity:** Agency-focused and harmonizing minority views exist (Lawson; Khorchide/von Stosch — **critiqued**). **Not identical** Muslim interpretation — UC **does not** flatten.

**UC does not claim** this dominant **R** reading is **wrong** to hold in Islam; UC **does not** adopt it as **UC B**.

### 4.5 UC doctrine: **Vindication of the Anointed** (**B**, **MD-02**)

**Explicit UC doctrine** ( **not** Abrahamic consensus, **not** “provisionally reconciled” **R**):

> After the **historical crucifixion**, God **did not leave Jesus to final defeat**. God **vindicated** the Anointed—**raising/exalting** him to divine presence (echoing Q 4:158 *rafaʿa* and Christian **R** resurrection proclamation in **function**, not **same narrative**).

**Defined terms:**

| Term | UC meaning |
|------|------------|
| **Crucifixion** | Historical Roman execution (**H**) |
| **Vindication** | God’s **definitive** act reversing shame/defeat — **faith** (**B**) |
| **Resurrection (Christian R)** | **Normative Christian** claim that God **raised** Jesus bodily — UC **B** **aligns in vindication** without requiring Nicene **soteriology** |
| **4:157 (dominant R)** | Denial Jesus was crucified — **C** between **R** Islam and UC **H+B** |

**UC does not** claim wording of 4:157 **alone** proves substitution, appearance-only, or UC vindication. **UC B** is **constructive**, informed by 4:158 and passion **witness**, **against** claiming Muslims already accept it.

**Soteriological bracket (MD-12 Option A):** UC **B** vindication **does not** specify **how** sins are remitted through the cross. UC **does not** teach **penal substitution**, **divine satisfaction**, or **blood sacrifice** as **binding B**. Members seek **forgiveness** from **One Sovereign** through **repentance** and faithful life; the Anointed’s **vindication** **witnesses** that God **does not** abandon the faithful to final shame.

### 4.6 What remains uncertain

- Exact **7th-century** audience exegesis of 4:157 (**H**/**R** history of interpretation).
- **H** cannot** prove or disprove **B** vindication (supernatural act).

### 5.3 Canon Conflict Resolution Procedure (CCRP) — **MD-03**, **MD-11**

**Authoritative procedure:** [ccrp-adopted.md](./ccrp-adopted.md) (Phase 6.10, semantics corrected Phase 6.11). This §5.3 **summarizes** the adopted text; if wording diverges, **ccrp-adopted.md** governs.

**Anti-circularity rule:** The Constitution **cannot** settle a scriptural dispute by citing **only** its own supremacy clause. UC **B** text is **not** independent proof of historical **H** or inherited **R** claims.

#### 5.3.1 Ruling status (mandatory at opening)

Decompose each dispute into sub-questions **q1…qn**. For each, record **claim class** (or governance question), **RS** status, **outcome kind** if **RS-1**, and **3A–3C** separately ([ccrp-adopted.md §0](./ccrp-adopted.md#0-four-dimensions-do-not-conflate), [§1](./ccrp-adopted.md#1-ruling-status-mandatory-at-opening)).

| Code | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **RS-1** | **Settled outcome** for sub-question scope — tag **outcome kind**: **B** (doctrine), **H** (historical baseline), or **G** (governance/procedure/pedagogy/language) |
| **RS-2** | Sub-question **explicitly open (C)** — e.g. **MD-06** / **DR-10** preservation theology |
| **RS-3** | Reopening active — freeze new **substantive B** / new **H baseline** on that sub-question |
| **RS-4** | Unaddressed |

**Kinds:** **MD-07**/**MD-08**/**MD-09**/**MD-10**/**MD-11** bind as **RS-1 G** on governance sub-questions only—not substantive **B** on theology under review. Full rules: [ccrp-adopted.md §1.4](./ccrp-adopted.md#14-outcome-kind-required-when-rs-1).

#### 5.3.2 Canon authority modes (**MD-10**)

See [glossary](./glossary-unified-covenant.md). Summary:

| Mode | UC role |
|------|---------|
| **Inclusion (B)** | Tanakh, NT, Qur’an in Unified Canon (**DR-06**) |
| **Witness (B + procedural)** | Layers used in UC narrative/worship/CCRP—**layer-specific function**, not identical **R** authority |
| **UC doctrine (B)** | Membership essentials (**§14**) |
| **Source-tradition (R)** | Authority **inside** Judaism, Christianity, Islam—not auto UC **B** |
| **Binding UC teaching (procedural + B flag)** | CCRP outcomes for **official** instruction |

**Receive (Declaration):** UC **includes** canonical **witnesses** under **CCRP**; **does not** adopt every **R** claim of inspiration, inerrancy, or perfect textual uniformity; **DR-10** **Sufficient Witness **B**** (§11). UC recognition **≠** source traditions’ recognition of UC interpretation.

**Anti-dismissal:** “Witness” requires **published** handling of textual claims (**H/R/B/C**), not ignoring inconvenient passages.

#### 5.3.3 Decision authority, evidence (**SD-***), and inference

Separate **three** layers in every step-4/5 record (full rules: [ccrp-adopted.md §3](./ccrp-adopted.md#3-decision-authority-evidence-provenance-and-inference)):

| Layer | Content |
|-------|---------|
| **3A Decision authority** | Governing **MD**/**DR**/**§**, class, **scope**, reopen rule — **not** **SD-*** for subject-matter **H**/**R** |
| **3B Evidence provenance** | **SD-*** on scripture, texts, scholarship (**SD-PRI** … **SD-INA** — see [glossary](./glossary-unified-covenant.md)) |
| **3C Argument** | Inference, assumptions, counterevidence, uncertainty |

**Rules:** UC **B**/**H** **does not** prove historical **H** or inherited **R**. **MD**/**DR** text is **not** **SD-PRI** for the theology/history under dispute (only for “what UC decided” documentary questions). **RS-2** → **no new B**. **SD-EXI**/**SD-INA** cannot support **new B** or high-confidence conclusions. **DR-10** strict **SD-EXC**/**SD-ART** requirements unchanged.

#### 5.3.4 Operational steps 1–6

1. **Register** — Passages, translation, genres, **claim types**, **RS** per sub-question.  
2. **Context** — Language, setting, intertexts, chronicle/registry **H** limits.  
3. **Tradition survey** — Dominant **R** + attested minority + period/school + **strongest objection** + **ranking uncertainty** if needed (**SD-*** cited).  
4. **Publish alternatives** — ≥2 serious options; **3A** + **3B** (**SD-***) + **3C**; conflicting scholarship **both** listed.  
5. **UC ruling proposal** — **B** text (if permitted), costs, classification; maintainer/council per §10; **MD-05** supersession optics check.  
6. **Record** — Traceability ID; **dissent**; **3A**/**3B**/**3C** summary; revision trigger.

#### 5.3.5 Outcomes, deadlock, teaching layers (**MD-08**, **MD-09**)

| Outcome | Binds? |
|---------|--------|
| Theological **C** | No **B** on topic |
| **Official UC teaching** | Yes, when CCRP **binding** flag set |
| **UC practice rule** | **B** + §12 if new |
| **Private inherited R** | No, if §7.3 allows; **public** teaching must not contradict essential **H**/**B** |
| **Constitutional amendment** | §12 only |

**Deadlock:** No consensus → default **C**; no new **B** on **RS-2** sub-questions. **H vs R** on past events: **H** binds history curriculum; **R** taught comparatively. **Split council:** **B** only with minority report.

**MD-09 (pedagogy, not DR-10 B):** Official instruction on preservation/*taḥrīf* must fairly present **R** diversity, apply **SD-*** rules, state evidential limits, and **not** imply a **B** preservation ruling while **MD-06** open.

**Worked example (RS-1 / MD-02):** Q 4:157 vs passion — **H** crucifixion; **R** documented; **B** vindication §4.5; **not** reconciled **R** harmony.

## §6 — Muhammad and prophetic finality (**MD-04**)

### 6.1 Qur’an 33:40 (**R** text)

Muhammad is **rasūl Allāh** and **khātam al-nabiyyīn** (Q 33:40). **Dominant Muslim R:** **Final prophet** — prophethood **closed** until the Hour (al-Ṭabarī tafsīr summary; `quran-33-40-seal-prophets`).

**Diversity:** Lexical *khātam*/*khātim*; academic debate on **development** of finality (Friedmann — `friedmann-finality-prophethood`; **contested**). **UC does not** treat minority **Bahā’ī** or harmonizing views as Muslim mainstream.

### 6.2 UC doctrine — **Seal of the Abrahamic canonical series** (**B**, **not** Islamic R)

**Approved formulation (**MD-04**):**

> Muhammad is **God’s prophet** to the 7th-century Arabs and **khātam al-nabiyyīn** in UC **specific sense**: the **seal of the Abrahamic canonical series** embodied in the **Unified Canon** (Tanakh → NT → Qur’an). **No further canonical book** joins this series except by **§10** revision.

**What this is:**

- A claim about **UC scriptural closure** and **recognition** of Muhammad’s prophetic office in UC narrative.
- **Inspired-by** Q 33:40 language, **reinterpreted** for UC structure.

**What this is **not**:**

- **Not** equivalent to **dominant Islamic** “no prophet after Muhammad” in **every** sense Muslims hold.
- **Not** a ruling that **Jesus** is non-central or that **Torah** is obsolete.
- **Not** a claim that **Muslims accept** UC wording.

**Prophecy after Muhammad in UC:** **Personal inspiration**, **study**, **ethical discernment** may continue; **UC does not** expect a **new canonical prophet** or **new scripture layer** without revision. **Wālī**, Spirit-discernment, or **ongoing rabbinic/halakhic** authority in **other** communities are **outside** UC **B**.

## §7 — Covenant and religious community (**MD-05**)

### 7.1 UC as **new** *brit* community (**B**)

UC is the **Unified Covenant assembly**—a **voluntary** religious body with its own **membership creed**, **Unified Canon**, and **CCRP**.

**UC explicitly rejects:**

- Replacing, fulfilling, or superseding **Judaism**, **Christianity**, or **Islam**.
- Cancelling **Jewish election** or **inheriting Israel’s covenant by default**.
- Treating other religions as **merely incomplete UC**.

**Affirms:** **Integrity** of Jewish, Christian, and Muslim communities and their **R** authorities **within themselves**.

### 7.2 Learning vs incorporating vs claiming authority

| Act | UC stance |
|-----|-----------|
| **Learn from** a tradition | **Encouraged** |
| **Include text** in Unified Canon | **B** — with CCRP |
| **Claim that tradition’s covenant/ecclesial authority over UC** | **Rejected** |

### 7.3 Membership and teaching (**MD-08**)

**Conversion** = assent to **essential B** (§14 register; traceability), public **covenant entry**, commitment to **CCRP** outcomes.

**Teaching and representation (minimum — MD-08):** Persons teaching or representing UC **officially** must present UC **H**/**B** **accurately**, document major **R** disagreements **without** misrepresenting Jewish, Christian, or Muslim beliefs, and **not** claim **R** reconciliation where **MD** forbids it.

**Distinctions (not collapsed):**

| Layer | Rule |
|-------|------|
| **Official UC doctrine** | **§14** essentials + CCRP **binding** teaching |
| **Accurate presentation** | Required of **official** teachers/representatives |
| **Public worship** | **No** universal requirement in this phase that every member **verbally affirm** all **B** in every service |
| **Personal belief** | Private **R** may differ where §7.3 allows |
| **Permissible dissent** | Recorded; must not contradict essential **H**/**B** in **official** UC teaching |

**May remain disputed (recorded dissent):** Details of atonement theory; private views on 4:157 **R** if **official** teaching respects **H** + **B**; halakhic/sharīʿa observance **personally** unless conflicting with **B** ethics.

**Shared gathering liturgy:** **MD-13** adopts a defined form as **B** (§8.2). **MD-08** still applies: no universal line-by-line verbal affirmation test; silent or partial participation and absence from a given service are **not** automatic grounds for expulsion unless another **B** duty applies.

**Future changes** to binding worship or calendar **B** require **§12** and maintainer or council decision.

**Departure:** Without coercion; no theological equivalence required.

**Dual identity:** Permitted **only** where conscience and **R** authorities allow; **conflict of loyalties** must be **acknowledged**, not hidden.

## §9 — Salvation, judgment, and ultimate destiny

**UC B (positive, limited):**

- **Moral judgment** is real; **repentance** meaningful before death.
- **Hope** of **resurrection/vindication** for the faithful; **mercy** of One Sovereign is **ultimate** but not permissive of unrepented cruelty.
- **Jesus’ vindication** (§4.5) is **prototype** of God’s faithfulness—not a claim that **only** Christians or **only** a specific atonement theory saves.

**Not claimed as A:** One path, one soteriology, identical eschatology across Judaism, Christianity, Islam.

**Forgiveness and the cross (MD-12 Option A):** Personal **forgiveness** is **not** asserted to occur **by mechanism X** disclosed in UC **B**; the **Anointed’s** passion and **vindication** **model** trust under affliction and **ground hope** that God **vindicates** the faithful, without requiring Nicene or substitutionary **R** formulas. **Official pastoral teaching (**G**, **MD-15**):** [uc-pastoral-salvation-guide.md](./uc-pastoral-salvation-guide.md). **UQ-03** mechanics remain **C**.

**Nicene salvation mechanics:** **R** Christian; **not** UC **B** requirement in full.

## §11 — Scriptural preservation and *taḥrīf* (**MD-06**, **MD-09**, **DR-10**)

**UC position (**B**, Phase 8.3):** **Sufficient Witness**

One Sovereign has preserved **adequate continuity** in the Tanakh and New Testament as **Unified Canon receives them**, and in the Qur’an as **Unified Canon receives it**, so that together they remain **fit witnesses** for covenant faith, moral instruction, and worship under **CCRP**. Textual variation, translation, and interpretive disagreement **do not** make any canonical layer **morally useless** or **wholly unreliable** as witness. Qur’anic language about distortion and concealment testifies to **misuse and covenant failure** in history (**R** witness UC respects); UC **does not** teach that those passages require belief in **wholesale fabrication** of the earlier layers. UC **does not** teach **perfect letter-for-letter uniformity** of every manuscript tradition as **B**.

**MD-09 (pedagogy):** UC **teaches** competing **R** positions and **H** manuscript facts **comparatively**, with **SD-*** discipline — consistent with **Sufficient Witness**, not as hidden reversal of **B**.

| Level | UC teaching |
|-------|-------------|
| **Manuscript variation (H)** | Attested; informs study |
| **Translation / interpretation** | Divergent; **CCRP** governs **B** use |
| **Theological preservation claims (R)** | Heard fairly; **B** floor = Sufficient Witness |
| ***Taḥrīf* discourse** | Multiple **R** meanings; UC **B** ≠ wholesale polemic |

**Rule (unchanged):** Textual variation **alone** **does not** prove or disprove **R** theology; UC **B** rests on **covenant reception**.

**Research:** [phase-6-5-tahrif-and-scriptural-preservation.md](../research/phase-6-5-tahrif-and-scriptural-preservation.md) — **H** input, not override of **B** without **§12**.

## Declaration of faith (v0.3)

We affirm **One Sovereign God**, Merciful and Just, without partner or rival.

We follow **Jesus the Anointed**, uniquely focal in our covenant story—crucified in history, **vindicated by God**, **not** the Nicene Second Person **as UC dogma**.

We receive **Tanakh, New Testament, and Qur’an** as **Unified Canon**—as **canonical witnesses** interpreted under **CCRP** (**MD-10**), trustworthy under **Sufficient Witness** (**DR-10**). This **does not** adopt every source tradition’s claims about inspiration, inerrancy, or perfect textual uniformity. Our recognition of these texts **does not** imply that Judaism, Christianity, or Islam recognises the Covenant’s interpretation of them.

We honour **Muhammad** as prophet and **seal of the Abrahamic canonical series in UC** — **our** doctrine, **not** a claim that Muslims adopt this formula.

We live in **accountability, justice, and mercy**, as **Unified Covenant** — a **new community** alongside, not replacing, Judaism, Christianity, and Islam.
