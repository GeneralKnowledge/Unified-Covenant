# Part I — Identity: one new Abrahamic religion

UC is a **voluntary *brit*** community: one Sovereign, one canonical witness tradition (Tanakh, New Testament, Qur’an under **CCRP**), one focal story centred on **Jesus the Anointed** and **Muhammad as seal of the UC canonical series**, one ethical covenant, and one **binding shared gathering liturgy** (**MD-13**). It is **not** Judaism, Christianity, or Islam, and it does **not** fulfil or supersede them.

The religion’s intellectual method remains **preservation-first** (**MD-07**): inherit revelation wherever defensible; reconcile where honest argument allows; **choose** where inheritance splits irreconcilably — and record the cost. Unity is **constructive**, not the pretence that three normative theologies were always one.

---

# Part II — God and ultimate reality

## One Sovereign (**ADOPTED B**, DR-01)

UC confesses **One Sovereign God** — Merciful and Just — **creator** of heaven and earth, **without partner or rival** (*shirk* rejected). God is **not** an indifferent first cause nor a tribal deity confined to one people’s imagination alone; God is the **living authority** who **speaks in history**, **forms covenant**, **judges** nations and persons, and **shows mercy** to the repentant.

**Coherence claim (WORKING):** Mercy and justice are **one character**, not alternating moods. God’s mercy does not cancel moral seriousness; God’s justice is not cruelty without remedy. UC rejects both a permissive “cheap grace” and a grim deity who delights in condemnation.

## Transcendence, presence, knowledge (**WORKING**)

God **transcends** creation — no image, no local containment, no division of divine being into rivals. God is also **present** to prayer, to conscience, to the afflicted, and to the gathered assembly. UC does **not** resolve every philosophical dispute (simplicity, timelessness, divine suffering); it **affirms** that God **hears** sincere prayer and **acts** in ways appropriate to covenant — without Nicene metaphysics as **B**.

## God and history (**ADOPTED H** + **WORKING**)

God **acted** in Israel’s story, in the mission of Jesus, and in the prophetic recitation through Muhammad. UC teaches these as **real divine communications**, not mere human projections — while distinguishing **H** reconstruction (what historians can say about manuscripts and events) from **R** claims (what communities confess) and **B** (what UC requires).

**Cost:** Muslims who hold absolute finality of Qur’anic wording in every **R** sense may reject UC’s layered canon; Nicene Christians may reject UC’s refusal of eternal Sonship; Jews may reject messianic centrality of Jesus. UC records these without caricature.

# Part III — Humanity, purpose, and moral failure

## Purpose and dignity (**PROPOSAL**)

Human beings exist to **know God**, **serve the Sovereign’s justice and mercy**, and **care for one another** as bearers of responsibility — not as gods, not as disposable matter. Dignity is **covenantal**: grounded in God’s address to humanity, not in autonomous self-definition alone.

## Wrongdoing (**PROPOSAL**)

**Sin** (UC term) is **failure of trust and duty** toward God and neighbour — including idolatry (*shirk*), cruelty, deceit, indifference to the vulnerable, and the **hardening** of habit against repentance. UC distinguishes:

| Kind | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Ignorance** | Wrong done without full understanding — still harmful; requires teaching and repair |
| **Weakness** | Recurrent failure where will is divided — requires discipline and mercy |
| **Injustice** | Harm to others — requires **restitution** where possible |
| **Defiance** | Knowing rejection of God’s claim — gravest; still not beyond reach of repentance unless final hardness |

UC **rejects** total depravity as **B** (humans are not incapable of good) and **rejects** moral self-sufficiency (no one is perfect before God).

## Repentance, forgiveness, transformation (**PROPOSAL**)

**Repentance** (*teshuvah* / turning) is **whole-life reorientation** — confession, amended conduct, repair to victims, renewed trust in God. **Forgiveness** is sought from **One Sovereign** and, where applicable, from those harmed; UC does **not** teach that human forgiveness alone replaces divine judgment.

**Restitution** where possible; **mercy** where repair is incomplete. Transformation is **expected** over time — not instant perfection.

# Part IV — Revelation, scripture, and prophetic authority

## Unified Canon (**ADOPTED B**, DR-06, MD-03, MD-10)

UC **receives** Tanakh, New Testament, and Qur’an as **canonical witnesses** — layered, each with **distinct function** in UC narrative and worship, interpreted under **CCRP**. “Receive” means **witness under UC procedure**, not adoption of every **R** claim about inspiration, inerrancy, or oral Torah authority.

## Reading when texts conflict (**WORKING** + CCRP)

When passages appear to clash:

1. **Classify** — **H**, **R**, **B**, **C** (v0.4.2 §2).
2. **Context** — genre, audience, translation, internal diversity.
3. **Strongest defensible** readings of each stream (**MD-07**).
4. **Moral coherence** with DR-01, DR-12 — scripture is not read to bless cruelty.
5. If irreconciliable in the same sense — **record**; adopt **B** only with explicit ruling.

**Reason** and **historical inquiry** inform UC; they do **not** automatically veto **B** faith in divine speech — but UC will not claim **H** where evidence is weak.

## Preservation and *taḥrīf* (**OPEN C**, DR-10, MD-06)

**ADOPTED:** Manuscript variation exists (**H**); competing **R** preservation theologies; UC **does not** teach wholesale *taḥrīf* polemic or its denial as **B**.

**PROPOSAL (future MD-06 resolution):** UC adopt a **modest B** — “God has preserved **sufficient witness** for covenant faith and moral instruction; no layer is **morally useless** because of variation” — **without** asserting perfect letter-by-letter preservation of every tradition’s **R** standard. **Not adopted in v0.5.**

## Muhammad and prophetic series (**ADOPTED B**, DR-08)

Muhammad is **prophet** and **seal of the Abrahamic canonical series in UC** — closure of **scriptural witness layers** for this community, **not** a claim that Muslims must accept UC formula or that prophecy never continues in non-canonical forms elsewhere.

# Part V — Jesus, Muhammad, and the unity of the faith

## Jesus the Anointed (**ADOPTED B**, DR-02–04, MD-02, MD-12)

**Identity:** Uniquely **focal** agent — teacher, servant, Anointed — through whom UC especially discerns God’s mercy and claim upon history. **Not** Nicene Second Person as **B**. **Not** a prophet interchangeable with others in UC story.

**Crucifixion (**H**):** Historical Roman execution under Pilate.

**Vindication (**B**):** God **did not** leave Jesus to final defeat; God **vindicated** the Anointed — exaltation to divine presence (**MD-12** bracket: **no** penal substitution, satisfaction, or blood sacrifice as **B**; **UQ-03** mechanics **OPEN**).

**Spiritual role (WORKING):** Jesus is **pattern** of trust under affliction, **witness** to One Sovereign’s faithfulness, and **centre** of UC retelling — not sole owner of God’s mercy toward all humanity in every **R** sense.

## Muhammad (**ADOPTED B**, DR-08)

**Prophetic role:** Final **canonical** voice in UC’s three-layer witness; emphasis on *tawḥīd*, justice, and completion of the **series** UC reads as one story. UC **does not** treat Muhammad as decorative honouring nor as replacement for Jesus’s focal role.

## One faith? (**ADOPTED MD-05** + **WORKING**)

UC is **one religion** with **one Sovereign** and **one community** — **not** three religions pretending to agree. Jewish, Christian, and Muslim **R** theologies remain **distinct** outside UC; UC **learns** without **claiming** their institutional assent.

# Part VI — Salvation, judgment, and ultimate destiny

## What UC affirms (**ADOPTED** + **WORKING**)

- **Moral judgment** is real; **repentance** matters before death.
- **Hope** of **vindication/resurrection** for the faithful; God’s **mercy** is ultimate but not permissive of unrepented cruelty.
- **Jesus’ vindication** prototypes God’s faithfulness (**DR-04**); **not** exclusive soteriology of Nicene or substitutionary type (**MD-12**).

## PROPOSAL: UC soteriological shape (not yet **B**)

UC teach that **salvation** — being **right with God and fit for vindicated life** — comes through:

1. **Faith** — trust in One Sovereign, not self-idolatry;
2. **Repentance** and amended life;
3. **Embodied mercy and justice** (DR-12);
4. **Participation** in the covenant community’s worship and mutual obligation — **without** universal attendance or line-by-line affirmation tests (**MD-08**).

The **cross** is **H** event and **witness** to innocent suffering and divine vindication; UC **does not** bind a **mechanism** of remission (**UQ-03** **OPEN**). **Forgiveness** is **from God** through repentance and faithful life — consistent with MD-12.

## Death, judgment, afterlife (**PROPOSAL**)

UC expects **accountability** beyond death and **hope** of **resurrection** in God’s mercy — without detailed geography of hell/heaven as **B** beyond “judgment is real; mercy is possible for the repentant.” **Incompatibilities** with specific **R** eschatologies are **acknowledged**, not erased.

# Part VII — Ethics and the covenantal life

## Foundations (**ADOPTED DR-12** + **PROPOSAL** system)

Ethics flow from **God’s character** (mercy + justice) and **covenant membership**: humans are **answerable**, **capable of repair**, and **called to protect the vulnerable**.

| Principle | UC direction |
|-----------|--------------|
| **Justice–mercy paired** | Neither harsh legalism nor sentimental excuse |
| **Honesty** | Truth-telling; false witness condemned |
| **Humility** | Before God and before the stranger |
| **Courage** | Witness against oppression “in our name” |
| **Generosity** | Material care, not only sentiment |
| **Fidelity** | Promises, covenants, family duty within conscience bounds |

## Inherited law (**ADOPTED** posture)

Halakha, sharīʿa, and Christian canon law are **R** in source communities — **not** auto-binding in UC. UC derives **principles** from scripture via CCRP; **contested** rules require **reasoned B** or remain **G**/local **O**.

## Conflict of rules (**WORKING** method)

When scriptural norms clash or embed ancient context: (1) identify **moral principle**; (2) distinguish **enduring** vs **contingent** application; (3) prefer readings that **minimise harm** and **maximise mercy** without nullifying justice; (4) **dissent** may be recorded — not every case yields one rule.

# Part VIII — Prayer, worship, sacred time, and ritual

## Theological purpose (**WORKING**)

Prayer is **address to One Sovereign** — gratitude, confession, intercession, listening. Communal worship **forms** character, **re-tells** covenant story, and **binds** members in shared duty. Study is **worship of the mind**; charity **worship of the hands**.

## Shared gathering (**ADOPTED B**, MD-13)

Authoritative text: [uc-shared-gathering-liturgy.md](./uc-shared-gathering-liturgy.md). **Seven-part order** and **spoken text** are **B**; lectionary, timing, translations are **G**. **MD-08:** vocal repetition **not** required; presence and silence valid.

**Rationale (WORKING):** The liturgy ** rehearses** UC identity each gathering — non-supersession, One Sovereign, repentance, Anointed vindication, Muhammad as seal formula, witness reading, justice commitment.

## Daily and weekly rhythm (**PROPOSAL**)

| Practice | Status |
|----------|--------|
| Morning/evening brief prayer (One Sovereign, mercy, duty) | **PROPOSAL G** |
| Personal scripture reading | **PROPOSAL O** encouraged |
| Weekly shared liturgy | **ADOPTED B** when community gathers |
| Charity / service | **ADOPTED B** ethic; **PROPOSAL G** minimum expectations |

## Annual calendar (**PROPOSAL** — not approved)

See [phase-7-7-annual-observances-proposal.md](./phase-7-7-annual-observances-proposal.md). **PROPOSAL preference:** one **Covenant Renewal Day** as future **B** anchor — timing **undecided**. **Do not** adopt Passover/Easter/Ramaḍān as UC holy days.

## Weekly sacred day (**OPEN**, MD-14)

**PROPOSAL preference (not adopted):** **No universal B weekly day** — local **G** scheduling — best for **MD-05** and dispersed communities. See [phase-7-7-md-14-weekly-day-brief.md](./phase-7-7-md-14-weekly-day-brief.md). **Maintainer must rule MD-14.**

## Life events (**PROPOSAL** sketches)

- **Birth/naming:** blessing in Sovereign’s name; **G**
- **Covenant entry:** assent to essential **B**, Declaration, community witness — **ADOPTED** pattern from §14
- **Marriage:** covenant of fidelity and mercy; **PROPOSAL G** ceremony using shared liturgy elements
- **Death:** prayer of trust and judgment/hope; **PROPOSAL G**

# Part IX — Community, belonging, and authority

## Belonging (**ADOPTED** + **WORKING**)

**Conversion** = assent to essential **B** (§14), public covenant entry, commitment to **CCRP** outcomes. **Not** comprehensive belief examination (**MD-08**). **Dual identity** permitted where conscience and **R** authorities allow — conflicts **acknowledged**.

## Mutual obligation (**PROPOSAL**)

Members owe one another: **truthful teaching**, **material care where able**, **correction without cruelty**, **protection of vulnerable**, **honest representation** of UC to outsiders (**MD-08**).

## Leadership (**WORKING**)

Formative phase: repository **maintainer** + ad hoc interpretive panels + **CCRP** records. Future council per v0.4.2 §10. **No** new hierarchy invented in v0.5.

## Children (**PROPOSAL**)

Raise children in covenant story, liturgy, and ethics; **adult assent** required for full membership — no coercion of conscience.
