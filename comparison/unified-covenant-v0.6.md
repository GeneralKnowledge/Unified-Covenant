# Part I — Identity: one new Abrahamic religion

UC is a **voluntary *brit*** community: one Sovereign, one canonical witness tradition (Tanakh, New Testament, Qur’an under **CCRP**), one focal story centred on **Jesus the Anointed** and **Muhammad as seal of the UC canonical series**, one ethical covenant, and one **binding shared gathering liturgy** (**MD-13**). It is **not** Judaism, Christianity, or Islam, and it does **not** fulfil or supersede them.

**Phase 8.2 integration claim (WORKING):** UC’s **God** (merciful justice) shapes how **scripture** is trusted; scripture shapes **ethics** and **repentance**; **salvation hope** shapes **prayer**; **sacred time** rehearses the whole; **liturgy** expresses communal identity each **Covenant Day** (proposed).

The intellectual method remains **preservation-first** (**MD-07**): inherit revelation wherever defensible; reconcile where honest argument allows; **choose** where inheritance splits irreconcilably — and record the cost.

# Part II — God and ultimate reality

*(Unchanged adopted core from v0.5.)*

## One Sovereign (**ADOPTED B**, DR-01)

UC confesses **One Sovereign God** — Merciful and Just — **creator**, **without partner or rival** (*shirk* rejected). God **speaks in history**, **forms covenant**, **judges**, and **shows mercy** to the repentant.

**Coherence (WORKING):** Mercy and justice are **one character**. UC rejects cheap grace and cruel condemnation without remedy.

## Transcendence, presence, knowledge (**WORKING**)

God **transcends** creation; God **hears** sincere prayer and **acts** in covenant-appropriate ways — without Nicene metaphysics as **B**.

## God and history (**ADOPTED H** + **WORKING**)

God **acted** in Israel’s story, in Jesus’ mission, and in the prophetic recitation through Muhammad. UC distinguishes **H**, **R**, and **B** (v0.4.2 §2).

**Revelation link:** Because God is **trustworthy**, UC can teach **Sufficient Witness** (PROPOSAL **B**) — not because manuscripts are magically identical, but because **covenant reception** under One Sovereign is ** warranted** (Part IV).

# Part III — Humanity, purpose, and moral failure

## Purpose and dignity (**PROPOSAL**)

Humans exist to **know God**, **serve justice and mercy**, and **care for one another** as responsible agents.

## Wrongdoing (**PROPOSAL**)

**Sin** = failure of trust and duty — *shirk*, cruelty, deceit, indifference, hardened habit. Categories: ignorance, weakness, injustice, defiance (see v0.5).

## Repentance, forgiveness, transformation (**PROPOSAL** + pastoral **G**)

**Repentance** is turning with confession, amended conduct, and **restitution** where possible. **Forgiveness** is from **One Sovereign** and, where applicable, from those harmed — see [uc-pastoral-salvation-guide.md](./uc-pastoral-salvation-guide.md).

**Ethics link:** Repair obligations in Part VII are **the hands of repentance**; calendar **Day of Turning** (PROPOSAL **G**) supports the same arc.

# Part IV — Revelation, scripture, and prophetic authority

## Unified Canon (**ADOPTED B**, DR-06, MD-03, MD-10)

UC **receives** Tanakh, New Testament, and Qur’an as **canonical witnesses** — layered, distinct functions, **CCRP** interpretation.

## Reading when texts conflict (**WORKING** + CCRP)

Classify **H/R/B/C**; context; strongest defensible readings (**MD-07**); moral coherence with DR-01, DR-12; record irreconcilables.

**Preservation PROPOSAL affects step 3:** Default trust in **witness function** unless **CCRP** case proves a reading **morally unusable** — not default suspicion of a layer.

## Preservation and *taḥrīf* (**OPEN C** → **PROPOSAL B**, DR-10, MD-06)

**ADOPTED today:** Manuscript variation (**H**); competing **R** preservation theologies; UC **does not** teach wholesale *taḥrīf* polemic or its denial as **B** (**MD-06** **C**).

**PROPOSAL (Phase 8.2 — Sufficient Witness):** Adopt as **B**:

> One Sovereign has preserved **adequate continuity** in the Tanakh and New Testament as **Unified Canon receives them**, and in the Qur’an as **Unified Canon receives it**, so that together they remain **fit witnesses** for covenant faith, moral instruction, and worship under **CCRP**. Textual variation, translation, and interpretive disagreement **do not** make any canonical layer **morally useless** or **wholly unreliable** as witness. Qur’anic language about distortion and concealment testifies to **misuse and covenant failure** (**R** witness UC respects); UC **does not** teach that those passages require belief in **wholesale fabrication** of the earlier layers.

**Full reasoning:** [phase-8-2-scriptural-preservation-proposal.md](./phase-8-2-scriptural-preservation-proposal.md).

| Layer | UC teaching posture (PROPOSAL) |
|-------|--------------------------------|
| **Tanakh** | Covenant witness; **H** variants in study; not oral Torah **B** |
| **New Testament** | Jesus witness; **H** passion; not inerrancy **B** |
| **Qur’an** | *Tawḥīd*, justice, series seal; not popular wholesale-*taḥrīf* **B** |

**Not adopted until maintainer closes MD-06.**

## Muhammad and prophetic series (**ADOPTED B**, DR-08)

Muhammad is **prophet** and **seal of the Abrahamic canonical series in UC** — closure of **scriptural witness layers** for this community.

# Part VI — Salvation, judgment, and pastoral life

## Adopted limits

- **Moral judgment** real; **repentance** matters.  
- **Vindication/resurrection hope**; mercy not permissive of unrepented cruelty.  
- **MD-12:** vindication **B**; substitution mechanism **not** **B**.  
- **UQ-03:** remission **mechanism** **C**.

## PROPOSAL: UC salvation shape (pastoral **G**, not new mechanism **B**)

**Salvation** = right relation to God through:

1. **Faith** in One Sovereign  
2. **Repentance** and amended life  
3. **Repair** and **mercy** toward others  
4. **Covenant participation** (**MD-08**)

The **cross** is **H** event and **witness**; **forgiveness** is **from God** through repentance and faithful life — **without** **B** blood-payment mechanism.

**Concrete cases:** serious wrongdoing, incomplete repair, victims, doubt — see [uc-pastoral-salvation-guide.md](./uc-pastoral-salvation-guide.md).

## Death, judgment, afterlife (**PROPOSAL**)

Accountability beyond death; **hope of resurrection**; judgment real; mercy for the repentant — without detailed **B** geography.

## PROPOSAL: Bound **UQ-03**

**Preferred:** Keep mechanics **C**; adopt pastoral guide as **G** so **UQ-03** means “no **B** mechanism,” not “UC silent on repentance.”

# Part VII — Ethics and the covenantal life

## Foundations (**ADOPTED DR-12** + **PROPOSAL** practice system)

Ethics flow from **God’s character** and **covenant membership**. Principles: dignity, truth, justice, mercy, responsibility, restitution, forgiveness, protection of vulnerable — **paired** justice–mercy.

**Inherited law:** Halakha, sharīʿa, Christian canon law = **R** in source communities — **not** auto-binding in UC.

## PROPOSAL: Moral reasoning method

Five-step method and worked cases: [uc-ethics-practice-guide.md](./uc-ethics-practice-guide.md).

**Integration:**

- **Scripture** supplies witness under **Sufficient Witness** (PROPOSAL) — not every verse applied literally without **CCRP**.  
- **Repentance** requires **restitution** when ethics demands it (Part III, pastoral guide).  
- **Liturgy** justice commitment (**MD-13**) = weekly ethical pledge.
