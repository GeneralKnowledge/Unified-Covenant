Unified Covenant (UC) is a **new Abrahamic religion**: one community under **One Sovereign**, reading **Tanakh, New Testament, and Qur’an** as canonical witnesses, centred on **Jesus the Anointed** and honouring **Muhammad as seal of the canonical series**. UC is **not** Judaism, Christianity, or Islam, and does **not** replace them (**MD-05**). What follows is what UC **believes and practises** — in plain language — with **open questions** named honestly where they remain.

---

## 1. God — the One Sovereign

UC believes in **One Sovereign God** — in inherited speech, *YHWH* or *Allāh* — who **created** heaven and earth, who is **without partner or rival** (*shirk* is rejected), and whose character joins **mercy and justice** as one, not as opposites that cancel each other.

God is **greater than** creation: no image, no division into rival powers. God is **near enough** to hear prayer, to confront injustice, and to **speak in history** through covenant and prophets.

God **judges** persons and nations with moral seriousness. God **shows mercy** to the turning heart. UC does **not** teach a permissive god who ignores cruelty, nor a cruel god who delights in condemnation.

**What UC does not require as membership dogma:** Nicene Trinity as metaphysics; rabbinic exclusivity of Israel’s covenant as UC’s whole story; every detail of Islamic *tawḥīd* theology as UC formulary — while **learning from** each tradition fairly (**MD-08**).

---

## 2. Human purpose and dignity

Human beings are **addressed by God** — responsible agents, not gods, not disposable matter. UC teaches that we exist to **know and serve** the Sovereign, to **worship** (with heart, mind, and hands), and to **care for neighbours and creation**.

**Dignity** is covenantal: grounded in God’s call, expressed in **accountability**, **capacity for repair**, and **duty toward the vulnerable** (**DR-12**).

Children are **raised in the covenant story**; **adult assent** enters full membership without coercion of conscience (**MD-08**).

---

## 3. What has gone wrong — sin and injustice

**Sin** is failure of **trust and duty** toward God and neighbour: *shirk* (giving ultimate loyalty to something other than One Sovereign), cruelty, deceit, indifference to the afflicted, and **hardened habit** that refuses to turn.

UC distinguishes **ignorance**, **weakness**, **injustice toward others**, and **defiance**. All require response; **injustice** especially requires **repair** where possible.

Humans are **not** incapable of good; no one is **perfect** before God. UC rejects both moral hopelessness and moral self-sufficiency.

---

## 4. Revelation — three witnesses and Sufficient Witness

UC **receives** the **Tanakh**, the **New Testament**, and the **Qur’an** as **canonical witnesses** — three layers of one covenant story, read under the **Canon Conflict Resolution Procedure** (**CCRP**, **DR-07**). “Receive” means **witness under UC discipline**, not automatic adoption of every inherited claim about inspiration, oral Torah, or inerrancy (**MD-10**).

| Witness | Distinctive role in UC |
|---------|------------------------|
| **Tanakh** | One Sovereign, covenant with Israel, prophets, law-as-witness |
| **New Testament** | Jesus the Anointed, **historical passion**, vindication hope, apostolic witness |
| **Qur’an** | *Tawḥīd*, justice, accountability; **Muhammad** and closure of the **UC canonical series** |

**Sufficient Witness (**B**, **MD-06**, **DR-10`):** One Sovereign has preserved **adequate continuity** in these texts as UC receives them, so they remain **fit witnesses** for faith, moral instruction, and worship. **Textual variation**, translation, and interpretive disagreement **do not** make any layer **morally useless** or **wholly unreliable**. UC **does not** teach **wholesale corruption** of earlier scriptures as doctrine, nor **perfect letter-for-letter uniformity** of every manuscript tradition.

Qur’anic language about **distortion and concealment** is heard as witness to **misuse and covenant failure** in history; UC **does not** treat it as a mandate to dismiss the Tanakh or New Testament as fabrications.

When passages **conflict**, UC **classifies** historical (**H**), inherited (**R**), binding (**B**), and open (**C**) claims; applies context and the **preservation-first method** (**MD-07**); and **records** what remains irreconcilable rather than pretending harmony.

---

## 5. Jesus the Anointed

**Jesus the Anointed** (*Māshíaḥ* / *al-Masīḥ*) is God’s **uniquely focal** agent in UC’s story: teacher, servant, centre of retelling — **not** the Nicene Second Person as UC dogma, **not** a prophet interchangeable with others.

UC **teaches as history (**H**)**: Jesus was **crucified** under Roman authority (Pontius Pilate).

UC **teaches as doctrine (**B**)**: God **did not** abandon Jesus to final defeat; God **vindicated** the Anointed — exalted to divine presence and faithful hope (**DR-04**, **MD-12**).

The **cross** is **real suffering** and **witness** to innocent affliction; UC **does not** bind **penal substitution**, **blood payment**, or satisfaction theories as the **mechanism** of forgiveness (**UQ-03** remains **C** on mechanics). **Vindication** grounds **hope** that God sees the faithful sufferer — without claiming UC has mapped every detail of how God forgives.

Dominant Muslim readings of Q. 4:157 and Christian passion theologies are **described fairly**; UC **does not** require members to adjudicate those **R** disputes as UC **B**.

---

## 6. Muhammad — prophet and seal of the series

UC honours **Muhammad** as **prophet** and **seal of the Abrahamic canonical series in UC** (**DR-08**): the **final canonical voice** in the three-layer witness for **this community**. That is **UC doctrine**, not a claim that Muslims must adopt UC’s formula or that prophecy never occurs elsewhere in other **R** senses.

Muhammad’s witness ** reinforces** *tawḥīd*, justice, and accountability. It **does not** replace Jesus’s **focal** role in UC worship and narrative.

---

## 7. Salvation — repentance, mercy, and open mechanics

**Salvation** — being **right with God** and **oriented toward vindicated life** — comes through:

1. **Faith** — trust in One Sovereign, refusing *shirk*;  
2. **Repentance** — turning, confession, **amended conduct**;  
3. **Restitution** — repair to those harmed, where possible;  
4. **Mercy** — received from God; extended with wisdom toward others;  
5. **Covenant participation** — worship and mutual obligation without a comprehensive belief examination (**MD-08**).

**Forgiveness** is sought from **God** and, where applicable, from victims. **Judgment** after death is **real**; **hope of resurrection** in mercy is **real**. UC does **not** bind detailed maps of hell and heaven as **B**.

Official pastoral teaching: [uc-pastoral-salvation-guide.md](./uc-pastoral-salvation-guide.md) (**G**, **MD-15**). **Mechanism of atonement** beyond vindication and repentance shape: **unresolved (**C**, **UQ-03**).

---

## 8. How to live — ethics in the world

Ethics flow from **God’s character** and **covenant membership** (**DR-12**): **justice and mercy paired**, truthfulness, humility, courage against oppression, generosity, fidelity, **protection of the vulnerable**.

Halakha, sharīʿa, and Christian canon law remain **R** in their communities — **not** automatically UC law. UC reasons from **scriptural witness** via **CCRP** and adopted **B**.

Official teaching guide: [uc-ethics-practice-guide.md](./uc-ethics-practice-guide.md) (**G**, **MD-15**) — a **moral framework**, not a comprehensive legal code.

**In family and household:** honesty, care, discipline without cruelty, teaching children the covenant story.

**In work and wealth:** fair dealing; refusal to defraud; **charity** as worship of the hands; resistance to oppression “in our name.”

**In conflict:** protect the vulnerable first; pursue truth; accept lawful accountability; **mercy** in restoration only where **justice** and **safety** allow.

**Toward outsiders:** **MD-05** — no mockery of Jewish, Christian, or Muslim **R** life; honest invitation without supersession; **neighbor-study** may occur near inherited seasons (**O**) without UC claiming those days as **B** obligations.

**When wrongdoing occurs:** stop harm; confess; repair; accept consequences; seek divine mercy — see pastoral guide for abuse, victims, and incomplete repair.

## 9. Worship and sacred time

### Binding shared liturgy (**B**, **MD-13**)

When the assembly gathers, UC uses the **seven-part shared liturgy** in [uc-shared-gathering-liturgy.md](./uc-shared-gathering-liturgy.md). Members may participate aloud, silently, or partially (**MD-08**). This is **UC worship**, not synagogue *tefillah*, Eucharistic liturgy, or *ṣalāt*.

### Daily (**G**)

Morning and evening address to One Sovereign — see [uc-foundational-practices.md](./uc-foundational-practices.md).

### Weekly — Covenant Day (**B**, **MD-14**)

**Sunday** is UC’s **normal** day for **communal assembly**, **shared liturgy**, **teaching**, and **rest from ordinary labour where practicable**. Communities **may adjust** the weekday when Sunday is impracticable (**G**). Covenant Day **does not** replace or invalidate **Shabbat**, Christian observances, or **Jumuʿah** (**MD-05**).

### Annual calendar (**MD-15**)

| Observance | Status | When |
|------------|--------|------|
| **Covenant Renewal Day** | **B** | Second **Sunday of October** |
| **Day of Turning** | **G** (strongly commended) | Sunday **before** Covenant Renewal |
| **Day of Witness to the Afflicted** | **G** | Second **Sunday of March** |
| **Neighbor-study days** | **O** | Educational; not UC holy days |

## 10. Community — belonging, conscience, and safety

**Entry:** assent to essential **B** (§14 register), public **covenant entry**, commitment to **CCRP** outcomes.

**Conscience (**MD-08**):** no line-by-line verbal test at worship; **recorded dissent** on non-essentials permitted; **dual identity** only where conscience and **R** authorities allow — conflicts **acknowledged**.

**Mutual obligation:** truthful teaching, material care where able, correction without cruelty, protection of vulnerable, honest representation of UC to outsiders.

**Leadership:** formative phase — maintainer, panels, **CCRP** records; future council per v0.4.2 §10.

**Abuse and discipline:** safety of victims **before** cheap reconciliation; remove leaders who harm; **transparent** accountability; **mercy** toward the penitent **never** at the cost of endangering victims (ethics and pastoral guides).

## 12. Hope — judgment, resurrection, renewal

UC **confidently teaches:** moral **accountability**; **repentance** matters; God’s **mercy** is real; **resurrection hope** for the faithful; Jesus’s **vindication** as **prototype** of God’s faithfulness (**MD-12** limits preserved).

UC **does not** claim identical eschatology with Judaism, Christianity, or Islam. **Speculative detail** (timing, geography of reward and loss) remains **unsettled **B**** beyond “judgment is real; mercy is possible for the repentant.”
