# Historical chronicle

Long-form chapters reconstructing the Abrahamic religions and their relationships **before** any experimental theological merge.

| Chapter | Title | Status |
|---------|--------|--------|
| [01](./01-before-the-branches.md) | Before the Branches | Draft (Phase 1) |
| [02](./02-from-israel-to-judaism.md) | From Israel to Judaism | Draft (Phase 2) |

**Chapter 2 scope:** Israel and Judah, Assyrian and Babylonian catastrophes, exile and Persian Yehud, Torah and temple, Exodus/Sinai as memory, Second Temple diversity through the first century CE (not Christianity).

**Evidence labels** used in chapters:

| Label | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| **Well established** | Strong, convergent evidence |
| **Widely accepted interpretation** | Substantial scholarship; depends on reconstruction |
| **Contested** | Meaningful scholarly disagreement |
| **Religious tradition** | Claim within a scriptural/theological framework |
| **Unknown / speculative** | Insufficient evidence for confidence |
