## 1. Historical timeline (concise)

| Period | Evidence type | Christological shape |
|--------|---------------|---------------------|
| **30s CE** | Gospels (earliest layers), Paul | Jesus: Jewish prophet/messiah figure; **crucifixion**; proclamation of resurrection |
| **30s–70 CE** | Paul, hymns, devotion patterns | **Exalted Lord** language; baptism “in Christ”; **not** Nicene formula (Ch.3; Hurtado) |
| **70–100 CE** | Synoptic + John trajectory | Rising **high christology** in John (**ca. 90–110**, Ch.3 table) |
| **2nd–3rd c.** | Patristic debates | Logos, subordination, adoptionism, modalism — **contested** developments |
| **325 CE** | Nicene Creed | Son **homoousios** with Father; **true God from true God**; incarnate, suffered, rose (Dossetti ed.; earlychurchtexts.com) |
| **381–451** | Constantinopolitan / Chalcedonian trajectories | **Trinity** and **two natures** in mainstream **Religious tradition** (post-chronicle) |
| **7th c.** | Qur’an | ʿĪsā **rasūl** / **masīḥ**; rejects divine sonship (Q 5:17, 9:30 — Ch.4) |
| **Post-70 Judaism** | Rabbinic streams | Rejection of Jesus as messiah/incarnation (`jewish-rejection-jesus-messiah-normative`) |

**Chronicle rule:** Ch.3 **explicitly** forbids reading Nicene Trinity into the 30s CE.

## 3. What Nicene language actually asserts

From **Creed of Nicaea (325)** (Greek/Latin witnesses via earlychurchtexts.com; Dossetti critical ed. cited in fourthcentury.com):

- Son: **begotten not made**, **from the substance/essence of the Father**
- **God from God**, **true God from true God**
- **Homoousios** with the Father
- **Came down**, **was incarnate**, **was made human**, **suffered**, **rose third day**

**Scholarly nuance (Kinzig 2023):** *Homoousios* at Nicaea may function primarily to **exclude** the Son from the **created** order rather than specify full later Trinitarian metaphysics — but **normative Christianity** subsequently read it as **full divinity of the Son**.

**Rejected without qualification:** Treating “economic Trinity” (modes of action) as **adequate** to Nicene **ontological** consubstantiality — **not supported** as equivalent in patristic or confessional sources (Phase 6.3 finding).

## 4. Jewish divine unity and covenant (relevant slice)

- **YHWH–Israel brit** — God’s unity is **covenant-particular**, not generic (conv. §1 E).
- **Messianic expectation** — diverse Second Temple; **not** empty of content (Ch.2–3).
- **Post-70:** Torah-centered life; prophecy cessation **attributed** (Cohen — `rabbinic-judaism-post-70-preview`).
- **Objection to UC “Anointed”:** Jewish **R** does not treat Jesus as **salvation-historical center** — see rabbinic module.

## 5. Islamic *tawḥīd* and Jesus

- **Anti-*shirk* / anti-sonship** — direct Qur’anic polemic (Ch.4).
- **ʿĪsā** as **word/spirit from God** in Qur’anic idiom — **not** Nicene incarnation.
- **Diversity:** Sufi and philosophical elaborations of divine unity **post-Qur’an** — not fully in chronicle.

**Contact vs. equivalence:** Shared **rejection of polytheism** (motif **A**); **not** shared Christology.
