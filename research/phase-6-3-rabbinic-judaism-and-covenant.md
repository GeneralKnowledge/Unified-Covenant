## 2. Torah, oral tradition, and authority

**Classical rabbinic self-understanding (attributed):**

- **Mishnah Avot 1:1** (tradition): “Moses received Torah from Sinai and transmitted it to Joshua…”— chain legitimizing **oral** as well as written Torah.
- **Concept:** *Torah she-be-‘al peh* (oral Torah) interprets and completes written Torah — **binding** in normative Judaism.

**UC v0.2 (UC-09):**

- Tanakh = **foundation witness** in Unified Canon.
- **Oral Torah** = **R** within Jewish communities, **not binding on UC** unless revised in.

**Assessment:**

| Question | Verdict |
|----------|---------|
| Does UC **acknowledge** Tanakh as revelatory? | **Yes** (UC-09) |
| Does UC **preserve Judaism’s own model** of Torah authority? | **No** — by design UC **subordinates** oral/halakhic bindingness to UC Constitution |
| Is that **defensible** as UC **B** choice? | **Yes**, if labeled **new religion**, not Judaism |
| Is it **misleading** if implied as preserving Judaism? | **Yes** — **strong Jewish objection** (review Perspective A) **sustained** |

## 4. Strongest Jewish objections (research-grade)

1. **Torah reduction** — Tanakh as “layer” without oral Torah **mis-frames** normative Judaism (UC-09).
2. **Messiah** — UC Jesus language is **Christian-type** or **syncretic**, not rabbinic messianism.
3. **Community** — UC assembly **≠** *klal yisrael*; incorporation of scriptures **≠** continuity with covenant people.
4. **Authority** — UC-11 “examinable authority” **conflicts** with **binding halakhic** process for Jews who treat *psak* as obligatory.

**Not claimed:** That **no** Jew could ever find UC interesting — only that **normative rabbinic** self-understanding is **not preserved**.
