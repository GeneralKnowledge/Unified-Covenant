## Option A — Open question (keep MD-06 unchanged)

**Formulation:** UC **continues** **DR-10** as **C**. Members **may** hold private **R** views on preservation/*taḥrīf* **if** **public** **UC** **teaching** respects **H** (e.g. crucifixion) and **does not** **contradict** **binding** **B** elsewhere. **No** **membership** **question** on **taḥrīf**.

### Strongest support

| Type | Argument |
|------|----------|
| **H** | Manuscript record **does not** **by itself** **settle** **theology** (v0.3 §11 rule). |
| **R** | **Muslim**, **Jewish**, and **Christian** **positions** **are** **internally** **diverse**; **no** **single** **R** **candidate** **is** **neutral**. |
| **Procedural** | **MD-06** **already** **approved**; **Phase** **6.5** **confirms** **polysemy** of **Qur’anic** **language**. |

### Strongest counterarguments

- **Declaration** **“receive”** **Tanakh/NT/Qur’an** **sounds** **like** **full** **preservation** **to** **some** **hearers**.  
- **Deferred** **B** **may** **freeze** **distrust** **in** **one** **layer** **(traceability** **canon** **distrust** **note)**.  
- **CCRP** **cases** **touching** **Bible** **reliability** **lack** **explicit** **UC** **stance**.

### Relationship to traditions

| Tradition | Fit |
|-----------|-----|
| **Judaism** | **Does not** **require** UC **to** **affirm** **MT** **theology** — **acceptable** **silence** **for** **some** **Jews**; **others** **want** **explicit** **respect** **for** **Torah** **integrity**. |
| **Christianity** | **Similar** **split** **(inerrantist** **vs.** **critical)**. |
| **Islam** | **Popular** **textual** **taḥrīf** **not** **affirmed** — **cost** **for** **some** **Muslim** **converts**. |

### Preserves / rejects

- **Preserves:** **MD-06**; **minimal** **misrepresentation** **risk**; **research** **honesty**.  
- **Rejects / postpones:** **Any** **clear** **UC** **preservation** **theology**.

### Unified Canon & membership

- **Canon** **unchanged**; **trust** **by** **covenant** **choice**, **not** **theological** **guarantee**.  
- **Membership:** **No** **taḥrīf** **litmus** **test**.

### Misrepresentation risk

- **Lowest** **if** **teaching** **explicitly** **labels** **C** **(recommended** **wording** **in** **main** **research** **§10.4)**.

### v0.3 compatibility

- **Full** **compatibility** **with** **MD-01–MD-05**, **CCRP**, **non-supersession**.

### Confidence & gaps

- **Confidence:** **High** **as** **governance** **choice**; **Low** **as** **pastoral** **completeness**.  
- **Gaps:** **Member** **expectations** **unspecified**.

## Option B — Limited corruption claim (**B**)

**Formulation (example — would need maintainer precision):** UC **teaches** **as** **B** that **some** **People** **of** **the** **Book** **in** **late** **antiquity** **and** **early** **Islam** **distorted** **scripture** **through** **interpretation**, **concealment**, **and** **selective** **legal** **use** (**Q** **4:46;** **5:13–15;** **2:75–79** **as** **witness**); UC **does** **not** **teach** **wholesale** **loss** **of** **the** **Torah** **or** **Gospel** **archetype** **as** **H** **fact**.

### Strongest support

| Type | Argument |
|------|----------|
| **R** | **Aligns** **with** **many** **Qur’anic** **accusations** **without** **I-1** **extremes**. |
| **H** | **Reynolds**/**Ṭabarī** **strand** **(maʿnā** **/** **misplaced** **words)** **fits** **narrow** **wording**. |
| **UC** | **Explains** **why** **CCRP** **needed** **without** **rejecting** **NT/Tanakh** **layers**. |

### Strongest counterarguments

- **Still** **imports** **Islamic** **polemic** **categories** **as** **UC** **B** — **Jewish** **R** **may** **experience** **as** **supersessionary** **accusation**.  
- **“Some** **People** **of** **the** **Book”** **historically** **targets** **Jews** **in** **tafsīr** — **antisemitism** **risk** **if** **careless**.  
- **Q** **5:43–48** **tension** **remains** **(positive** **Torah/Injīl)**.

### Traditions

- **Islam:** **Closer** **to** **moderate** **R** **not** **I-1**.  
- **Judaism/Christianity:** **Partial** **rejection** **expected**.

### Preserves / rejects

- **Preserves:** **Unified** **Canon** **inclusion** **with** **critique** **of** **misuse**.  
- **Rejects:** **Pure** **P1** **Christian/Jewish** **preservation** **narratives** **as** **UC** **norm**.

### Canon & membership

- **Essential** **affirmation** **of** **limited** **distortion** **B**? **Risky** — **may** **become** **de** **facto** **Muslim-leaning** **test**.

### Misrepresentation risk

- **Medium–high** **for** **Judaism** **if** **historical** **“Jews** **of** **Medina”** **generalised**.

### v0.3 compatibility

- **Compatible** **if** **worded** **with** **MD-05** **non-supersession** **and** **strong** **anti-generalisation** **clause**.

### Confidence & gaps

- **Confidence:** **Medium** **theologically**; **Low** **politically**.  
- **Gaps:** **Need** **Arabic** **primary** **tafsīr** **verification** **beyond** **thesis** **summary**.

## Option C — Preservation with interpretive divergence (**B**)

**Formulation (example):** UC **teaches** **as** **B** that **God** **has** **preserved** **substantial** **continuity** **of** **the** **Abrahamic** **scriptural** **witness** **in** **the** **Tanakh** **and** **New** **Testament** **as** **UC** **receives** **them**, **such** **that** **they** **remain** **fit** **for** **covenant** **instruction** **under** **CCRP**; **divergence** **among** **communities** ** arises** **primarily** **through** **interpretation**, **translation**, **canon** **selection**, **and** **historical** **context**, **not** **through** **wholesale** **fabrication** **of** **those** **layers** **in** **the** **7th** **century**. UC **acknowledges** **Qur’anic** **accusations** **of** **distortion/concealment** **as** **R** **witness** **to** **misuse**, **not** **as** **automatic** **H** **proof** **of** **lost** **archetypes**.

### Strongest support

| Type | Argument |
|------|----------|
| **H** | **Manuscript** **continuity** **to** **early** **Islam** **(MT,** **NT** **traditions)**. |
| **R** | **Harmonises** **with** **Q** **5:43–48** **(guidance** **remains)** **and** **Jewish/Christian** **providence** **language** **(qualified)**. |
| **UC** | **Makes** **“receive** **…** **as** **Unified** **Canon”** **coherent** **without** **Nicene** **inerrancy**. |

### Strongest counterarguments

- **Many** **Muslims** **hold** **textual** **taḥrīf** **as** **R** — **Option** **C** **contradicts** **I-1** **without** **full** **argument** **in** **every** **community**.  
- **Sounds** **Christian/Jewish-leaning** **to** **some** **Muslim** **readers**.  
- **“Substantial** **continuity”** **is** **vague** — **CCRP** **may** **need** **verse-level** **rules**.

### Traditions

- **Judaism/Christianity:** **Broad** **welcome** **(except** **strict** **inerrantists** **wanting** **stronger** **wording)**.  
- **Islam:** **Partial** **—** **conflicts** **with** **popular** **R** **unless** **nuanced** **teaching** **on** **maʿnā** **vs** **naṣṣ**.

### Preserves / rejects

- **Preserves:** **Layered** **canon**; **historical** **crucifixion** **H**; **interfaith** **study**.  
- **Rejects:** **Wholesale** **UC** **affirmation** **of** **textual** **corruption** **B**.

### Canon & membership

- **Could** **be** **essential** **(soft** **preservation** **affirmation)** **or** **recommended** **only**.

### Misrepresentation risk

- **Medium** **for** **Islam** **if** **presented** **as** **“what** **Qur’an** **really** **means”** **rather** **than** **UC** **B**.

### v0.3 compatibility

- **Strong** **fit** **with** **MD-03**, **MD-06** **distinctions**, **Declaration**.

### Confidence & gaps

- **Confidence:** **Medium** **overall** **—** **best** **if** **community** **needs** **one** **B** **sentence** **on** **canon** **trust**.  
- **Gaps:** **Define** **“substantial”**; **Injīl** **vs** **Gospels** **mapping**.

## Option D — Stronger theological adjudication (**B**)

**Two** **variants** **(maintainer** **must** **choose** **one** **pole** **only**):

| Variant | Claim |
|---------|--------|
| **D1 — Strong preservation** | UC **affirms** **inspired** **integrity** **of** **Tanakh** **+** **NT** **(inerrancy-lite** **or** **full** **inerrancy)** **as** **B**. |
| **D2 — Strong corruption** | UC **affirms** **significant** **textual** **corruption** **of** **pre-Qur’anic** **scriptures** **as** **B** **(I-1/I-2** **family)**. |

### Strongest support

- **D1:** **Clarity** **for** **Christians** **/ some** **Jews**; **matches** **“receive”** **maximal** **reading**.  
- **D2:** **Clarity** **for** **Muslims** **with** **textual** **taḥrīf** **R**.

### Strongest counterarguments

- **D1:** **Collides** **with** **Qur’anic** **accusation** **literature** **unless** **heavily** **reinterpreted**; **H** **variants** **stress** **inerrantist** **claims**.  
- **D2:** **Undercuts** **UC** **canon** **(NT/Tanakh** **as** **witness)**; **H** **does** **not** **support** **wholesale** **rewrite** **theory**; **high** **supersession** **appearance** **despite** **MD-05**.

### Traditions

- **Each** **variant** **aligns** **one** **stream** **at** **cost** **of** **others**.

### Canon & membership

- **D2** **especially** **threatens** **DR-06** **coherence** **unless** **canon** **redefined** **(e.g.** **only** **“restored** **meaning”** **via** **Qur’an)** — **major** **constitutional** **change**.

### Misrepresentation risk

- **High** **for** **non-aligned** **traditions**.

### v0.3 compatibility

- **Poor** **unless** **paired** **with** **major** **revision** **of** **§5** **and** **Declaration**.

### Confidence & gaps

- **Confidence:** **Low** **for** **either** **variant** **as** **unified** **Abrahamic** **B**.  
- **Not** **recommended** **without** **explicit** **community** **acceptance** **of** **one-tradition** **alignment**.

## Option E — Procedural canon authority only (**B**, meta-doctrine)

**Formulation:** UC **does** **not** **rule** **DR-10** **theology** **(remains** **C** **for** **corruption/preservation** **claims)** **but** **adopts** **B** **meta-rule:** **Unified** **Canon** **layers** **are** **authoritative** **for** **UC** **because** **the** **covenant** **community** **so** **constitutes** **them** **under** **CCRP**, **analogous** **to** **constitutional** **law** **—** **not** **because** **UC** **has** **settled** **inherited** **R** **preservation** **debates**.

### Strongest support

- **Clarifies** **Declaration** **without** **picking** **I-1** **vs** **P1**.  
- **Matches** **MD-03** **Constitution** **as** **meta-layer**.

### Counterarguments

- **May** **feel** **secularising** **to** **devout** **members** **from** **all** **three** **streams**.  
- **Does** **not** **answer** **pastoral** **questions** **about** **Bible** **“errors.”**

### Confidence

- **Medium** **as** **supplement** **to** **A**, **not** **standalone** **complete** **answer**.

## Maintainer recommendation (not adopted)

**Primary recommendation:** **Retain Option A (MD-06)** **for** **this** **cycle**, **plus** **non-doctrinal** **clarifiers** **from** **research** **§10.4** **(wording** **suggestions** **only)** **to** **reduce** **ambiguity** **of** **“receive.”**

**If** **the** **maintainer** **requires** **a** **DR-10** **B** **before** **public** **launch** **or** **major** **outreach:** **Option C** **is** **the** **most** **defensible** **single-sentence** **theology** **given** **current** **evidence**, **provided**:

1. **Teaching** **explicitly** **states** **UC** **B** **≠** **dominant** **Muslim** **textual** **taḥrīf** **≠** **Christian** **inerrancy**.  
2. **CCRP** **handles** **verse-level** **conflicts** **without** **presuming** **corruption**.  
3. **Phase** **6.5** **gaps** **(full** **Ṭabarī/Reynolds** **read)** **are** **closed** **or** **acknowledged** **in** **traceability**.

**Do not recommend Option D** **variants** **for** **a** **tri-root** **UC** **without** **explicit** **one-tradition** **realignment**.

**Withhold** **strong** **recommendation** **for** **Option B** **as** **membership** **B** **until** **antisemitism** **and** **MD-05** **review** **complete**.
