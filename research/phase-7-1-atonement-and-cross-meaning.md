## 1. Executive question

Given **binding UC** claims:

- **H:** Crucifixion under Pilate (**DR-03**, **MD-02**)
- **B:** Vindication of the Anointed after crucifixion (**DR-04**, §4.5)
- **R:** Dominant Q 4:157 denial trajectory documented, **not** adopted (**DR-05**)

**What should UC members teach and practice** about:

1. **Forgiveness** of sins before God?
2. **Hope** after death?
3. **Meaning** of Jesus’ suffering on the cross (beyond bare **H** fact)?

**MD-02** already settles **H + vindication B**. It **does not** settle penal substitution, satisfaction, ransom, moral exemplar, or Qur’anic *kaffāra* analogies as **UC B**.

## 4. Inherited tradition readings (**R**, **3B**)

### 4.1 Nicene / mainstream Christian soteriology

| Element | Typical **R** claim | UC relation |
|---------|----------------------|-------------|
| Cross as **sacrifice** for sin | Central in many confessions | **R** witness; **not** UC **B** (**MD-01**, §9) |
| **Penal substitution** | Popular Protestant articulation | **R**; **C** for UC unless **MD-12** |
| **Resurrection** bodily | Dominant Easter **R** | UC **B** **aligns in vindication function** without requiring Nicene mechanics (§4.5) |
| **Eucharist** “remembrance” tied to passion | **R** liturgical | Feeds **UQ-02**, not settled here |

**Strongest objection to UC:** Without substitution or equivalent, UC “uses the cross” but **does not preach the gospel** (Christian **R** polemic).

**UC response (defensive, not adoption):** UC **B** is **vindication** + **Anointed centrality**, not Nicene soteriology; **MD-08** forbids forcing Nicene **B** on members.

### 4.2 Jewish frames (post-70 **R**)

| Element | **R** posture | UC relation |
|---------|---------------|-------------|
| Suffering servant motifs (Isaiah 53) | **Contested** messianic application | **R** midrash; UC may cite **witness** without claiming rabbinic adoption |
| Temple / sacrifice system | **R** halakhic | **Not** UC **B** |
| Repentance (*teshuvah*) before God | **R** + motif **A** | Compatible with UC §9 **repentance B** without cross mechanics |

**Strongest objection:** Elevating crucified Jesus as salvation prototype **repeats** messianic claims Judaism **R** rejects.

**UC response:** **MD-05** non-supersession; UC **does not** claim Torah-abrogating king or replacement **brit**.

### 4.3 Islamic frames (Qur’an + tafsīr **R**)

| Element | **R** posture | UC relation |
|---------|---------------|-------------|
| Q 4:157 — no crucifixion of Jesus (dominant) | **R** majority | **Documented**; UC **H** crucifixion **conflicts** at **R** level |
| Q 4:158 *rafaʿa* — exaltation | **R** | **Echoed** in UC vindication language (**B** constructive) |
| ʿĪsā as sinless messenger | **R** | **Informative**; UC **does not** require identical Christology |
| No “Son died for world” | **R** anti-sonship | **Aligns** with **MD-01** |

**Strongest objection:** Affirming crucifixion **H** and centering cross in narrative **contradicts** dominant Muslim **R**.

**UC response:** **MD-02** cost accepted; UC is **new** community, not **tawḥīd**-only Islam.

## 6. Adversarial deep-dives

### 6.1 “Vindication-only is enough”

**Pro:** **MD-02** closed **H+B** narrative; adding soteriology **B** risks **MD-01** Nicene drift or **MD-02** reopen.

**Con:** §9 promises **repentance** and **hope** without saying **how** the Anointed’s passion relates to member sin—a **catechetical hole**.

### 6.2 “Representative suffering must be **B**”

**Pro:** Unlocks prayer (“in his afflictions”), justice-mercy pairing, interfaith honesty (cross **means** something).

**Con:** Muslim and Jewish **R** may read any suffering-theology as **hidden** substitution; requires careful **G** teaching memos.

### 6.3 “Keep mechanics **C** forever”

**Pro:** **MD-08** minimalism; maximum room for converts’ private **R**.

**Con:** §1 “unified worship” + §9 **prototype** language **implies** more than constitution delivers.

## 8. Defensible UC teaching lines (non-binding sketches)

These are **research positions**, **not** adopted **B**:

1. **Clarification line:** “UC **B** vindication means God **honored** the Anointed after shameful death; personal **forgiveness** is sought from **One Sovereign** through **repentance**—the Anointed’s path **models** trust, not a disclosed penal mechanism.”
2. **Solidarity line (candidate B):** “God was **present in** the Anointed’s suffering; his faithfulness under affliction **binds** UC members to **mercy-justice** practice.”
3. **Bracket line:** “UC **does not** teach **how** the cross removes sin; it **teaches** **that** God vindicated the Anointed and **calls** members to **repentance** and **hope**.”
