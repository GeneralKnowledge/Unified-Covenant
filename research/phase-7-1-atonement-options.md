## Option A — Vindication-only clarification (editorial v0.4.x)

**Formulation (example):** Expand §4.5 and §9 to state plainly that UC **B** **does not** teach penal substitution, satisfaction, or Qur’anic substitution narratives; **forgiveness** is from **One Sovereign** through **repentance**; the Anointed’s **vindication** **confirms** God’s faithfulness to the one who suffered **H** crucifixion and **models** persevering trust.

### Strongest support

| Type | Argument |
|------|----------|
| **3A** | Fully within **MD-02** vindication **B** |
| **Pastoral** | Closes catechetical gap without new metaphysics |
| **Cost control** | Avoids **MD-01** Nicene drift |

### Strongest counterarguments

- May still feel **too Christian** for some Muslim associates.  
- Does **not** give liturgy a positive “meaning of cross” beyond prototype language.

### **MD-02** reopen?

**No** — if text **only** clarifies existing **DR-04** and §9 limits.

### §14 impact

**DR-04** unchanged; optional footnote in traceability only.

### v0.4 compatibility

**Full** with **MD-01**, **MD-08**, **MD-02**.

### Draft location

## Option B — Representative suffering **B** (narrow)

**Formulation (example — would need maintainer precision):**

> UC teaches **as B** that in the **historical suffering** of the Anointed on the cross, **One Sovereign** disclosed **solidarity with the afflicted** and **faithfulness** to the obedient servant; members are **called** to **carry mercy-justice** in the world **in union with** this pattern. UC **does not** teach **penal substitution**, **divine child sacrifice**, or that God **required** blood to forgive sins **as B**.

### Strongest support

| Type | Argument |
|------|----------|
| **Pastoral** | Connects cross to **DR-12** justice-mercy **B** |
| **Liturgical** | Enables memorial/hope language in **UQ-02** |
| **Theological** | Distinguishes UC from bare “Jesus died (H)” minimalism |

### Strongest counterarguments

- **Muslim R:** Any elevated suffering theology near cross **resembles** Christian passion piety.  
- **Jewish R:** Suffering servant **R** applied to Jesus remains **offensive**.  
- **MD-02 risk:** If framed as “what the cross **accomplished** salvifically,” maintainer must judge **reopen**.

### **MD-02** reopen?

**Possibly yes** if **DR-04** scope expands from vindication **after** death to **soteric function** of suffering. **Mitigation:** New **DR-13** row; **MD-12** documents non-reopen rationale.

### §14 impact

New essential **B** row likely (**DR-13** or expanded **DR-04** gloss).

### v0.4 compatibility

**Requires** **MD-12** + **§12**; likely **v0.5**.

### Draft location

[phase-7-2-contingent-amendments.md](../comparison/phase-7-2-contingent-amendments.md) § B.
