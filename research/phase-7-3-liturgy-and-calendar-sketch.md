# Phase 7.3 — Liturgy and calendar sketches (UQ-02)

**Status:** Research module. **Not** UC doctrine. **Does not** amend v0.4.

**Dependency:** UQ-03 ideally **clarified or bracketed** via **MD-12** before liturgy **B** adoption.

**Constraints:** **MD-08** (no universal worship affirmation test); **MD-05** (non-supersession); **MD-03** canon layers.

---

## 1. Problem statement

v0.4 §1 promises **one coherent worship**; §8 marks UC ritual calendar **C**; **MD-08** forbids forcing a detailed worship creed on members. **UQ-02** asks what **minimal B** (if any) makes UC **gatherable** without impersonating synagogue, mosque, or church **R** liturgy.

---

## 2. Non-goals

- **Not** replacing halakha, sharīʿa, or canonical Christian liturgy **R**.  
- **Not** mandating Eucharist, *salāt* five times, or full Jewish *daven* as UC **B**.  
- **Not** resolving **DR-10** preservation theology via worship choices.

---

## 3. Three archetypes (sketches)

### Archetype 1 — Shared Abrahamic prayer script

**Description:** Weekly gathering: fixed opening (*Shema*-adjacent monotheism + *Fatiḥa*-adjacent mercy idiom, **new UC wording**), scripture **rotation** (Tanakh / Gospel / Qur’an per week), silence, justice-mercy commitment, closing blessing.

| Dimension | Assessment |
|-----------|------------|
| **MD-05** optics | **Medium risk** — sounds “replacement” if marketed wrong |
| **MD-08** fit | **High** — single script optional for leaders, not conscience test |
| **Canon use** | **G** rules needed (which translations; **DR-10** **C** pastoral) |
| **UQ-03 link** | Memorial of Anointed **optional** corner; stronger if **MD-12** **A/B** |

### Archetype 2 — Layer-rotating liturgy

**Description:** Three-week cycle: **Week A** Tanakh-forward, **Week B** NT-forward (passion/vindication readings), **Week C** Qur’an-forward (*tawḥīd*, seal). Shared ethics homily; **no** claim that one week “is” Jewish/Christian/Muslim service.

| Dimension | Assessment |
|-----------|------------|
| **MD-05** optics | **Lower** — explicit rotation honors layers |
| **Pedagogy** | **High** — trains CCRP literacy |
| **Cost** | **High** leader preparation; Muslim **R** sensitivity on Week B crucifixion **H** |

### Archetype 3 — Minimal covenant assembly

**Description:** Monthly assembly: Declaration recitation, **open** prayer (member-led), one canon passage via CCRP-chosen **G** lectionary, service project. **No** fixed sacraments.

| Dimension | Assessment |
|-----------|------------|
| **MD-08** fit | **Highest** |
| **§1 “coherent worship”** | **Weakest** — may feel thin |
| **Scale** | Easiest for small communities |

## 4. Calendar sketch (non-binding)

| Occasion | Class | Sketch |
|----------|-------|--------|
| Weekly rest / gathering | **C** → candidate **B** | Friday sunset, Sunday, or neutral Saturday — **maintainer choice** |
| Passover / Easter / Ramaḍān proximity | **R** in source communities | UC **optional** study days, **not** claiming **R** observance |
| UC covenant renewal | **B** candidate | Annual Declaration + entry of new members |
